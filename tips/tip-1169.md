# TIP-1169: Card Records

## Abstract

A new precompile keeps card records: an issuer's settlement account (the payee of its holds) issues a card on a cardholder's account with a status, a spend limit, an expiry, a program type, a holder reference and a network token hash, and replaces it with a new credential that keeps the same limit and open holds. A new reservations function, `holdCard`, places a TIP-1153 hold through one card and refuses it when the card is not active, expired, replaced, over its limit or not allowed to hold the token; `holdCardStandIn` records a stand-in approval through a card the same way, except that the card's limit does not bound it. Merchant category, merchant, velocity and per-transaction-type controls stay with the issuer, which sets them and supplies their inputs.

## Motivation

TIP-1153 holds are keyed by authorization and know nothing about cards. Card state then lives only in the issuer's database: a cardholder's freeze binds the issuer only as far as the issuer honors it, several cards on one account share one mandate limit, a wallet cannot resolve a card or a device token to its account without asking the issuer, and a replaced card has no on-chain link to its successor.

This TIP puts on chain what binds the issuer to the cardholder's wishes and what a wallet must read without the issuer: owner, payee, status, limit, expiry, program type, holder reference, replacement and token hash. A hold through a card checks them. The cardholder's root key can freeze, unfreeze, close and lower the limit; nothing else changes a record except its payee.

Alternatives considered:

- **Controls on chain** (merchant categories, merchants, velocity, daily limits per transaction type). The issuer's key supplies the authorization context they would be checked against, so a compromised key could misstate a category; they catch issuer software faults but protect less than they seem, and cost counter writes on every hold and a slot per blocked merchant. Issuers keep them in their authorization path instead.
- **One status bit per card** (status only). Cheapest, but limits, expiry, replacement and token resolution would stay off chain.
- **Records inside TIP-1153.** Saves a cross-precompile read but makes TIP-1153 card-specific. This TIP adds three selectors to TIP-1153 and keeps the rest of it unchanged.

## Assumptions

- The cardholder's mandate key (TIP-1011, see TIP-1153) is scoped to `holdCard` rather than `hold`, to `holdCardStandIn` rather than `holdStandIn`, and, for a card on a credit line, to `holdCardWithLine` rather than `holdWithLine`. Only then are card status, expiry and limits binding on that key: a key that may also call `hold`, `holdStandIn` or `holdWithLine` can place holds outside its cards. Scoping costs nothing on chain; it is the cardholder's choice at grant, and this TIP does not change keychain scoping.
- The payee is the issuer's settlement account and signs record changes directly, as it calls `captureFor` and `refund`; issuer operator key rotation needs no record change.
- Card ids are opaque `bytes32` values the issuer chooses, such as `keccak256` of its processor card id. They are never derived from a card number: a BIN fixes most digits, so the rest of a hashed card number can be brute-forced in seconds. Card numbers, expiry dates, CVVs and last four digits never reach the chain.
- The network token identifier is a hash of the network's token reference (Visa's token reference ID, Mastercard's token unique reference), which is not a payment credential; never a hash of the device token (DPAN), which is a card number in form and would be reversible.
- The holder is a `bytes32` reference the issuer maps to its user or employee record, never a name.
- A record naming an owner who never granted a mandate is inert: holds still need the owner's access key.

# Specification

## Overview

A **line** is one card's state: owner, payee, status, program type, expiry, limit, committed amount, current credential, holder reference and token hash. A **credential** is a card id under a payee that maps to a line. `issueCard` creates a line with one credential; `replaceCard` maps a new credential to the same line, which becomes its current credential, and closes the old one. Open holds, the limit, the committed amount and the token hash belong to the line, so they follow a replacement with no per-hold writes.

A card is keyed by (payee, card id), so one issuer's ids never collide with or squat another's. Statuses are **Inactive** (issued, not yet usable), **Active**, **Frozen** and **Closed** (final). A replaced credential reads Closed with `replacedBy` set, whatever its line's status.

| Operation | Caller | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `issueCard`, `updateCard`, `replaceCard`, `setTokenHash` | payee | Write the payee's cards |
| `setProgramTokens` | payee | List the tokens the payee's cards may hold |
| `setCardStatus`, `lowerCardLimit` | owner, root key only | Freeze, unfreeze, close, lower the limit |
| `IReservations.holdCard`, `holdCardWithLine`, `holdCardStandIn` | owner (its mandate key) | Hold through a card |
| `getCard`, `cardByToken`, `programAllows` | anyone | Read |

## Mechanics

### Amounts, limits and time

Limits and committed amounts are `uint96` in the base units of the tokens a line holds. A line counts every token it holds in that token's units, so a limited card should hold tokens of one unit, such as USD stablecoins; Earn share legs count share units. A limit of `type(uint96).max` is no limit. `expiresAt` is a unix timestamp in seconds; zero never expires, and a line is expired once `block.timestamp >= expiresAt`. Any expiry may be set, including one already passed.

### issueCard(owner, card, status, limit, expiresAt, program, holder)

The caller is the payee. Reverts `InvalidCard` for a zero owner, a zero card id, status Closed or an out-of-range status or program, and `CardExists` when the payee already has a credential with that id (including a replaced one). Allocates the next line id (a counter from one), maps the credential to it, and writes owner, expiry, status, program, payee and limit, and the holder reference when non-zero. Emits `CardIssued`.

### updateCard(card, status, limit, expiresAt, program, holder)

The caller is the payee, and the card must be its line's current credential on a line that is not closed: `UnknownCard` for an unknown card under the caller, `CardClosed` for a replaced credential or a closed line, `InvalidCard` for an out-of-range status or program. Sets every issuer field; the payee may raise the limit, change status to any value including Inactive, and close the line. Emits `CardUpdated`.

### replaceCard(card, next)

The caller is the payee; `card` must be current on an open line (`UnknownCard`, `CardClosed`); `next` must be non-zero (`InvalidCard`) and unused under the payee (`CardExists`). Records `replacedBy[card] = next`, maps `next` to the line, makes it the line's current credential, sets the line Active, and points the line's token hash, if any, at `next`. Emits `CardReplaced`. A replacement is a new active card, as networks issue it; the payee may set another status afterwards.

### setTokenHash(card, tokenHash)

The caller is the payee; `card` must be current on an open line. A non-zero hash already mapped under the payee reverts `CardExists`. Removes the line's previous hash mapping, maps the new hash to the current card and stores it on the line; zero removes the hash. Emits `CardTokenSet`. `cardByToken` returns the line's current card, so a device token keeps resolving after a replacement.

### setProgramTokens(tokens)

The caller is the payee. Replaces its list of the tokens its cards may hold: an empty list allows every token, which is the default. The list is versioned by an epoch the call raises, so a new list needs no deletion of the old entries; a token is listed when its stored epoch equals the current one. Emits `ProgramTokensSet`.

### setCardStatus(payee, card, status) and lowerCardLimit(payee, card, limit)

Owner only, with its root key: `Unauthorized` when the owner is the transaction origin and an access key signed the transaction, or when the caller is not the line's owner; `UnknownCard` for an unknown card; `CardClosed` for a replaced credential or a closed line. `setCardStatus` allows Frozen and Active from Active or Frozen, and Closed from any status; Inactive, and Active from Inactive, revert `Unauthorized`, since activation is the payee's. `lowerCardLimit` reverts `Unauthorized` when the new limit exceeds the current one. Both emit `CardUpdated`.

### holdCard(token, id, amount, expiresAt, payee, issuer, card)

Added to TIP-1153's `IReservations`. `issuer` is the account the card is keyed under (its `ICards` payee); the hold's `payee` may differ, so a leg paying a facility on the issuer's behalf, such as an Earn savings facility that pays the issuer in cash at capture, still goes through the card. It runs `hold`'s checks in `hold`'s order (amount and expiry, token, payee resolution, TIP-403 authorization, an existing record) and then, for the card under `issuer`:

1. `UnknownCard` unless the credential exists and its line's owner is the caller.
2. `CardInactive` unless the credential is its line's current one, the line is Active and it is not expired.
3. `TokenNotAllowed` when the issuer's program lists tokens and not `token`.
4. `CardLimitExceeded` when the line has a limit and `committed + amount > limit`.

It then places the hold exactly as `hold` does, records the line on the authorization, adds `amount` to the line's committed amount, and emits `HoldPlaced` followed by `CardHoldPlaced`. A failure reverts the whole call.

### holdCardWithLine(token, id, amount, linePart, line, lender, expiresAt, payee, issuer, card)

Added to TIP-1153's `IReservations` beside `holdCard`, for a card whose purchases a TIP-1153 credit line funds, such as a credit card on an issuer's or a bank's line. It runs `hold`'s checks in `hold`'s order, then the four card checks above for the card under `issuer` with the whole `amount`, line part included (a card's limit bounds what it commits however it is funded), then `holdWithLine`'s checks (`InvalidAmount` unless `0 < linePart <= amount`, `InsufficientAvailable` when `amount - linePart` exceeds the owner's available balance, `LineNotFound` without the line in `token`, `CreditNotOffered` unless `payee` is the payee the credit is bound to, `InsufficientAvailable` past the credit's headroom). It then places the hold exactly as `holdWithLine` does, records the card's line on the authorization, adds `amount` to the line's committed amount, and emits `HoldPlaced`, `CardHoldPlaced` and `CreditHeld`, in that order. A failure reverts the whole call.

The hold is then both a card authorization and a line hold, and each rule applies unchanged: the card's committed amount follows the authorization's (its line part included), `adjust` up repeats the card checks before taking the increase from the owner's balance and then the credit, and captures, releases, `adjust` down and refunds never check the card while they draw, end and repay the line as TIP-1153 specifies. A refund that repays the line gives back the card's committed amount like any refund.

### holdCardStandIn(token, id, amount, expiresAt, payee, issuer, card)

Added to TIP-1153's `IReservations` beside `holdStandIn`, for an approval the issuer made in stand-in on a card. It runs `hold`'s checks in `hold`'s order, then the first three card checks above (`UnknownCard`, `CardInactive`, `TokenNotAllowed`) for the card under `issuer`, then records the approval exactly as `holdStandIn` does: it reserves `min(amount, available)`, possibly zero, marks the hold as a stand-in hold whose captures may owe up to the offline cap, records the card's line on the authorization, adds what the authorization commits to the line's committed amount, and emits `HoldPlaced` with the reserved amount, `CardHoldPlaced` and `StandInHoldPlaced` with `amount`, in that order. A failure reverts the whole call.

The card's status is checked when the approval reaches the chain, not when the issuer made it: a card frozen, closed, replaced or expired in between refuses it, as the reference card model declines a stand-in approval on a card that stopped being active. The card's limit is not checked. Stand-in approvals are bounded by the issuer's offline limit, which the owner accepted ahead of time through TIP-1153's offline cap; the issuer is already bound by the approval when it reaches the chain, and the card's limit, like the mandate's spending limits, binds approvals the issuer makes online. The approval still counts: the line's committed amount follows the authorization, so later holds through the card meet a limit the approval used, and an `adjust` up rechecks the card as for any card hold. The committed amount follows what the authorization commits (the reserved amount, then what is captured), not the approved amount, as the key limit does for stand-in holds in TIP-1153; a stand-in excess paid by `chargeStandIn` under another id is card-less, as `charge` is.

### Committed amount

A line's committed amount follows the committed amount of every authorization placed through it, as the key limit's `counted` does in TIP-1153: the live hold plus what was captured and not refunded. After every change to such an authorization its change of committed amount moves the line's: holds and increases add, a final capture above the hold adds its excess and one below gives back the difference, releases, expiries, decreases and refunds give back, a non-final capture changes nothing, and a further `charge` under a settled card authorization adds. Give-backs never take the counter below zero. The counter has no period: a card limit bounds what the card commits over its life, net of refunds.

### Checks on hold changes

`adjust` up on a card authorization repeats the line checks for the increase (Active, not expired, within the limit; `CardInactive` or `CardLimitExceeded`), so a frozen, closed or expired card's holds cannot grow. The credential is not rechecked: holds follow the line through replacements. Captures (`capture`, `capturePart`, `captureFor`, `captureForPart`), releases, `releaseExpired`, `adjust` down and refunds never check the card: open holds survive a freeze, a close or a replacement, and a card authorization keeps settling after its card closed.

### Card limits and the mandate key

`holdCard` and `holdCardWithLine` check the card whoever signs; the limit binds an access key only when the key can call neither `hold` nor `holdWithLine`. Scoping the mandate key's TIP-1011 call scope to `holdCard` and `holdCardWithLine` instead of `hold` and `holdWithLine` at grant makes card status, expiry and limits binding on that key at no storage cost, for holds its credit line funds as for the rest, so freezing a card stops both. `charge` and `chargeStandIn` stay card-less: they settle late presentments of purchases the card approved (offline or forced captures), which the card's status at presentment must not refuse, and remain bounded by the key's spending limit and the debt cap (or offline cap). A key scoped to `holdCardStandIn` instead of `holdStandIn` records stand-in approvals only through its cards, so a frozen, closed or expired card refuses them too; their amount is bounded by the offline cap rather than the card's limit. A key scoped to `holdCard` alone, without `holdCardWithLine`, places no line holds.

### Activation

The precompile exists only from T15 on Tempo L1, with `holdCard`, `holdCardWithLine` and `holdCardStandIn`, as TIP-1153. Before T15 neither is callable and nothing changes.

## Gas

The precompile charges standard calldata input cost and storage costs. Under TIP-1000 each new slot costs 250,000 gas, partly recoverable through TIP-1060 storage credits; a rewrite costs about 2,900 to 5,000 gas and a cold read 2,100.

| Operation | New slots | About |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Issue a card (credential, two line slots; the line counter is rewritten) | 3 | 750,000 gas |
| First card hold on a line (its committed counter) | 1 | 250,000 gas |
| A card hold by a mandate key with a spending limit | 0 beyond `hold`: the line id packs into the authorization slot that holds the key's `counted`, which such a hold writes anyway | 6 to 7 cold reads (credential, line status, current credential, program list, limit, committed), 1 rewrite |
| A card hold by the root key or a key without a limit | 1 beyond `hold` (that authorization slot), deleted with the record on a release with nothing captured | 250,000 gas |
| A card hold with a line part (`holdCardWithLine`) | 0 beyond `holdWithLine`: the line id packs beside the `line` flag, which every line hold writes | the card reads and counter write of a card hold |
| A stand-in approval through a card (`holdCardStandIn`) | 0 beyond `holdStandIn`: the line id packs beside the `standIn` flag, which every stand-in hold writes | a card hold's reads less the limit read, and its counter write |
| Freeze, unfreeze, close, change limit, expiry or program | 0 | about 5,000 gas |
| Replace (new credential, `replacedBy`, current credential the first time) | 2 or 3 | 500,000 to 750,000 gas |
| Provision a network token (hash mapping, hash on the line) | 2 | 500,000 gas |
| List tokens in a program | 1 per token new to the list, plus 1 for the list the first time | 250,000 gas each |

A card that is used therefore costs four slots, about 1,000,000 gas, before storage credits. The committed counter returns to zero when every authorization of a line is released with nothing captured; the next card hold writes it again. Issuance rewrites one counter slot shared by all issuers, so card issuance serializes in parallel block building; holds do not touch it.

## Interfaces

Address: `0x4341524400000000000000000000000000000000` (ASCII "CARD").

```solidity
interface ICards {
    enum Status { Inactive, Active, Frozen, Closed }
    enum Program { Debit, Prepaid, Credit, Corporate, Virtual }

    /// A card as getCard reads it: its line's fields, seen through one credential.
    struct Card {
        address owner;      // zero for an unknown card
        address payee;
        Status status;      // Closed for a replaced credential
        Program program;
        uint64 expiresAt;   // zero never expires
        uint96 limit;       // type(uint96).max is no limit
        uint96 committed;   // live holds plus captures net of refunds through the line
        bytes32 holder;     // the issuer's reference, never a name
        bytes32 current;    // the line's current credential; zero for an unknown card
        bytes32 replacedBy; // the credential that replaced this one; zero if current
        bytes32 tokenHash;  // hash of the network token reference; zero without one
        uint64 line;
    }

    function issueCard(address owner, bytes32 card, Status status, uint96 limit, uint64 expiresAt, Program program, bytes32 holder) external;
    function updateCard(bytes32 card, Status status, uint96 limit, uint64 expiresAt, Program program, bytes32 holder) external;
    function replaceCard(bytes32 card, bytes32 next) external;
    function setTokenHash(bytes32 card, bytes32 tokenHash) external;
    function setProgramTokens(address[] calldata tokens) external;
    function setCardStatus(address payee, bytes32 card, Status status) external;
    function lowerCardLimit(address payee, bytes32 card, uint96 limit) external;

    function getCard(address payee, bytes32 card) external view returns (Card memory);
    /// The line's current card; zero for an unknown hash.
    function cardByToken(address payee, bytes32 tokenHash) external view returns (bytes32 card);
    function programAllows(address payee, address token) external view returns (bool);

    event CardIssued(address indexed payee, bytes32 indexed card, address indexed owner, uint64 line);
    event CardUpdated(address indexed payee, bytes32 indexed card, Status status, uint96 limit, uint64 expiresAt, Program program);
    event CardReplaced(address indexed payee, bytes32 indexed card, bytes32 indexed next);
    event CardTokenSet(address indexed payee, bytes32 indexed card, bytes32 tokenHash);
    event ProgramTokensSet(address indexed payee, address[] tokens);

    error CardExists(); error UnknownCard(); error CardInactive(); error CardClosed();
    error CardLimitExceeded(); error TokenNotAllowed(); error InvalidCard(); error Unauthorized();
}

// Added to IReservations (TIP-1153); issuer keys the card, payee receives the hold:
function holdCard(address token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint64 expiresAt, address payee, address issuer, bytes32 card) external;
/// holdWithLine (TIP-1153) through the card: the card checks count the whole amount, line part included.
function holdCardWithLine(address token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint128 linePart, bytes32 line, address lender, uint64 expiresAt, address payee, address issuer, bytes32 card) external;
/// holdStandIn through the caller's card issued by issuer, after the card's checks other than its limit.
function holdCardStandIn(address token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint64 expiresAt, address payee, address issuer, bytes32 card) external;
/// Follows HoldPlaced for a hold placed through a card.
event CardHoldPlaced(address indexed owner, bytes32 indexed id, bytes32 indexed card, uint64 line);
```

`Unauthorized()` shares its selector with the TIP-1153 and TIP-20 errors of the same name.

Storage layout (precompile slots in declaration order):

| Slot | Field | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | credentials | `mapping(payee => mapping(card => uint64 line))` |
| 1 | lines | `mapping(uint64 => {address owner; uint64 expiresAt; uint8 status; uint8 program}, {address payee; uint96 limit}, {uint96 committed}, {bytes32 current}, {bytes32 holder}, {bytes32 tokenHash})` |
| 2 | lastLine | `uint64` |
| 3 | replacedBy | `mapping(payee => mapping(card => bytes32))` |
| 4 | tokens | `mapping(payee => mapping(tokenHash => bytes32 card))` |
| 5 | listed | `mapping(payee => mapping(token => uint32 epoch))` |
| 6 | programs | `mapping(payee => {uint32 epoch; bool restricted})` |

A line's first two slots are written at issue; `committed` on its first card hold, `current` on its first replacement, `holder` and `tokenHash` only when set. TIP-1153's authorization gains `uint64 cardLine` after `standIn`, in the same slot as `counted` and before the `line` flag; it is zero for authorizations not placed through a card.

# Compatibility

Before T15 nothing changes. TIP-1153 behavior is unchanged for every existing selector: `hold`, `holdStandIn` and `charge` stay card-less, and authorizations not placed through a card never read or write card state. Card programs adopt card records by issuing cards from their settlement accounts, holding through `holdCard` (`holdCardWithLine` for a card on a TIP-1153 credit line, `holdCardStandIn` for a stand-in approval), and having cardholders scope new mandate keys to those selectors. Clients detect support by calling `programAllows` (which answers on a node with this precompile) or by `holdCard` reverting `UnknownCard` for an unknown card. Indexers map holds to cards through `CardHoldPlaced` and card state through the `ICards` events.

# Observability

`CardIssued` (with the line id), `CardUpdated` (after any payee or owner change, with the resulting status, limit, expiry and program), `CardReplaced`, `CardTokenSet` and `ProgramTokensSet` record every record change; `CardHoldPlaced` links a hold to its card and line. A line's committed amount is read with `getCard`.

# Threat Model

| Actor | Controls | Enforcing check | On compromise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Issuer mandate key (scoped to `holdCard`, `holdCardWithLine` and `holdCardStandIn`) | holds through the owner's cards | the card's status, expiry, limit and program, plus TIP-1153's limits and caps | Holds only through active cards, within their limits except stand-in approvals, which the offline cap bounds; captures, releases and refunds as in TIP-1153 |
| Payee (issuer settlement account) | every field of its cards, its program list | caller must be the card's payee; closed is final | Can activate, raise limits and issue cards on accounts that granted it a mandate, so card limits bind the key but not a compromised settlement account; the mandate key's limit and the debt cap still bound what it spends |
| Cardholder root key | freeze, unfreeze, close, lower limit | root key, record owner | Already controls all funds |
| Anyone | reads | none | Reads link an account's cards publicly, as holds already link an account to its issuers |

The most consequential attack is an issuer key placing holds the cardholder froze or limited: with the key scoped to `holdCard`, `holdCardWithLine` and `holdCardStandIn`, every hold checks the card, including holds a credit line funds and stand-in approvals, and an increase rechecks it. A stand-in approval may pass the card's limit: what it reserves is bounded by the available balance and the key's spending limit, and what its capture owes past that by the owner's offline cap, which the key cannot change. A key that may still call `hold`, `holdStandIn` or `holdWithLine` bypasses its own cards; scoping is the cardholder's decision at grant. Category, merchant, velocity and per-type controls are the issuer's and are not enforced on chain: a compromised issuer key can misstate the context those checks need, so on-chain enforcement would protect less than it seems. Residual risk: records and events make an account's cards linkable; pattern privacy is a Zones question.

# Invariants

- Every credential maps to at most one line, and a line has exactly one current credential: the issued one until its first replacement, then the latest.
- A credential that was replaced reads Closed and is never current again; a Closed line never changes again.
- A hold placed through a card was placed while the card was its line's current credential, Active, unexpired, allowed to hold the token and within its limit including that hold.
- A card authorization's increase happened while its line was Active, unexpired and within its limit including the increase.
- A line's committed amount equals the sum over its authorizations of their live hold plus captured less refunded.
- `cardByToken` returns the current credential of the line holding that hash, and each hash maps to at most one line per payee.
- Only the payee and, within `setCardStatus` and `lowerCardLimit`, the owner's root key change a record.
