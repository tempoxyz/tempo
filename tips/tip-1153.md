# TIP-1153: Reservation Core

## Abstract

A precompile at `0x5245535600000000000000000000000000000000` keeps only what must be atomic with TIP-20 balances: holds, captures, charges, refunds, capped debt, pledges and liens. A hold moves part of an owner's balance out of its TIP-20 balance slot and reserves it for a payee until a hard expiry. Holds are placed by the owner or by a **holder**, a contract the owner gives a spend budget for a token. Every reservation is a two-slot record keyed by `(owner, placer, id)` and deleted when it settles; an owner's first live reservation in a token also writes a position slot, deleted with the last one. Card records, limits, mandates, multi-leg purchases, refund bookkeeping, credit lines and savings live in holder contracts, outside the protocol.

## Motivation

Card authorization needs funds set aside without moving them, a final amount that may differ from the authorization, and payments that follow it. Holding funds in place keeps custody with the owner and needs no issuer float. Everything else a card program does changes often and differs per program, so it belongs in upgradeable contracts; the protocol bounds what such a contract, or a key, can take from an owner's balance, and makes every movement follow the token's policy.

## Assumptions

- A holder is trusted up to its budget: what it may spend per period plus what it may hold at once. A buggy or compromised holder can take at most that, and holds and pledges it placed end at their expiry (31 days for a hold, 400 days for a pledge).
- Budgets, payee terms (debt caps, offline caps, capture tolerance, debt term) and owner pledges need the owner's root key; an access key cannot change them.
- Each placer's hold ids are its own namespace. A record's id is free again once it settles, so a placer that needs ids to stay unique (one per purchase) keeps that rule in its own storage.
- The core keeps no state for a settled record, so it neither bounds refunds by what was captured nor stops an id being reused. Placers carry both duties; see Placer obligations.

# Specification

## Records

A record is a hold, a pledge or a lien, stored in two slots keyed by `(owner, placer, id)`:

| Slot | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| head | `token` (20 bytes), `held` (u64, still reserved), `expiresAt` (u32; 0 only for a lien without expiry) |
| terms | `payee` (20 bytes), `captureLimit` (u64, what captures may still pay), `toleranceBps` (u16), `kind` (u8), `standIn` (bool) |

The record exists while `token` is non-zero. A final capture, a release, an expired release, and a pledge or lien seized or reduced to nothing delete both slots. Amounts are u128 in the ABI and must fit 64 bits (`InvalidAmount`).

| Kind | Placed by | Payee | Expiry | Adjust | Capture | Release |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hold (1) | owner (`hold`) or holder (`holdFrom`) | merchant | `now < expiresAt <= now + 31 days` | placer | placer or payee | placer or payee |
| pledge (2) | owner (`pledge`, root key) or the authority as holder (`pledgeFrom`) | authority | `now < expiresAt <= now + 400 days` | payee, down only | payee (seizure) | payee |
| lien (3) | holder of the token's `LIEN_ROLE`, root key | authority (the placer) | 0 (none) or in the future | payee, down only | payee, root key, with the role (seizure) | payee |

From `expiresAt`, `adjust` and `capture` revert `HoldExpired`; anyone may call `releaseExpired` (`HoldNotExpired` before). Calls name the record by owner, placer and id; a missing record reverts `HoldNotFound`, an occupied id `HoldExists`, any other caller `Unauthorized`.

## Budgets

`approveHolder(holder, token, limit, period)` (owner's root key) sets one slot per (owner, holder, token): `{limit u64, spent u64, outstanding u64, period u32, periodStart u32}`.

- `outstanding` is what the holder's live holds and pledges reserve. Placing or raising one draws on it; releasing, reducing or expiring returns to it.
- `spent` is what the holder's captures, charges and seizures paid in the current period. It is never returned. A capture moves what it pays from the hold out of `outstanding` into `spent`; a capture beyond the hold and a charge add to `spent` directly.
- Every draw needs `spent + outstanding + draw <= limit` (`BudgetExceeded`). A capture's excess beyond its hold needs the same room, even from a revoked holder.
- With `period > 0`, `spent` resets to 0 once `now >= periodStart + period`, and `periodStart` advances by whole periods. With `period = 0` the budget is a lifetime one. Changing `period` restarts it at `now`.
- `limit = 0` revokes: no new holds, raises, charges or pledges (`Unauthorized`). Existing holds still capture within what they hold, release and expire, and pledges still seize and release.
- `holdFrom`, `pledgeFrom` and a holder's `charge` check the caller's budget (`Unauthorized`, then `BudgetExceeded`) before the owner's payment policy and available balance, so a caller the owner never approved learns nothing about either.

## Holds

- `hold(request)` places on the caller's own balance; `holdFrom(owner, request)` places on `owner`'s balance within the caller's budget.
- The payee resolves through the address registry (TIP-1022); the payment check below must pass for owner to payee.
- An ordinary hold reserves `amount` (`InsufficientAvailable` above the available balance). A **stand-in** hold, for an amount approved offline, reserves `min(amount, available)`.
- `captureLimit = amount * (10000 + toleranceBps) / 10000`, with the tolerance the owner set for that payee and token at placement (default 0). Changing the payee's terms later does not change live holds.
- An owner-placed hold charges the signing access key (TIP-1011) its full capture limit, tolerance and stand-in shortfall included, since captures need no further key check. A holder's hold draws `amount` on its budget.
- `adjust(owner, placer, id, amount)` sets what a hold reserves. Raising it needs the increase available, rechecks the payment policy, draws the increase on the budget, and raises `captureLimit` to `amount * (1 + tolerance)` if that is higher, charging an owner's key for the raise. A stand-in's capture limit cannot be raised (`InvalidAmount`). Lowering it returns the difference.

## Capture

`capture(owner, placer, id, amount, isFinal)` pays the payee:

1. `amount <= captureLimit` (`CaptureLimitExceeded`); `amount = 0` only with `isFinal`.
2. The part up to `held` comes from the hold; the rest from the available balance, then from debt to the payee within the owner's caps (a stand-in's debt may reach the offline cap).
3. `captureLimit` drops by `amount`. A final capture releases what the hold still reserves and deletes the record.

A pledge or lien seizure pays only what the record still backs, never creating debt; seizing all of it deletes the record.

## Payment policy

Every movement out of an owner's balance (capture, charge, seizure, debt repayment) and every refund goes through one TIP-20 internal check: the token is not paused, the recipient is a valid direct recipient other than the sender, the TIP-403 policy authorizes the sender and the recipient, and the recipient's TIP-1028 receive policy accepts the sender. Placing a hold, raising it and placing a pledge run the same check. A failure reverts `PolicyForbids`; releases never check policy. Two exemptions waive only the sender's TIP-403 authorization:

- **A hold's held part.** A capture paid entirely from what an ordinary hold still backs pays its own payee, fixed when the hold was placed under the policy then in force. After a compliance burn took part of the hold, the part it took no longer counts: a capture that would draw on the owner's free balance in its place is a new payment and is checked. A merchant that delivered against an authorization is paid after the owner is blocked; anything beyond the hold, and every charge, pledge seizure, new hold or raise from a blocked owner, reverts. A hold lasts at most 31 days, which bounds this window; to stop it sooner, the placer or payee releases the hold, or the policy blocks the payee.
- **A lien seizure.** A lien is an issuer freeze; its authority seizes whatever the owner's status.

Compliance burns may take what holds reserve, never what pledges and liens hold. `burnBlocked` is one; `burnAt` is one only when the policy blocks the owner as a sender, and otherwise burns only the free balance (`InsufficientBalance` beyond it), so a bridge never burns reserved funds of an owner in good standing. The part taken is recorded as a shortfall on the owner's holds; ending holds consumes it first, so what remains still backs the others, while pledges and liens return or pay exactly what they hold. While a shortfall stands the owner places and raises no holds (`InsufficientAvailable`), so funds that arrive later never back the burned holds; pledges and liens, which the shortfall never reaches, still may. A capture from a hold returns its part to the balance first, shortfall included, and is exempt only when what came back covers the amount it pays.

## Charge and refund

- `charge(owner, token, id, amount, payee, standIn)`: the owner (charging its access key `amount`) or a holder (adding `amount` to `spent`). Pays from the available balance, then debt. No record; `id` is the placer's reference in `Charged`.
- `refund(owner, token, placer, id, amount)`: the caller pays `amount` back to `owner`, first forgiving what `owner` owes the caller in `token`, then moving the rest from the caller's available balance (`InsufficientAvailable`) under the payment check from caller to owner. `placer` and `id` name the hold it refunds, in `Refunded`.

## Debt

`setPayeeTerms(payee, token, {debtCap, offlineCap, toleranceBps, debtTerm})` (owner's root key) bounds what the owner may owe that payee: `debtCap`, or `max(debtCap, offlineCap)` for stand-in holds and charges (`DebtCapExceeded`, or `InsufficientAvailable` with no cap).

- Debt is kept per creditor, up to 4 creditors per (owner, token) (`TooManyCreditors`), one slot each, oldest first.
- A creditor's debt lapses `debtTerm` seconds after it was first incurred (0 or more than 365 days means 365 days). More debt to a standing creditor keeps its lapse time, so no debt stands longer than one term. A lapsed debt is dropped (`DebtLapsed`) the next time the owner's debts in that token are touched, or by anyone through `clearLapsedDebt`.
- After every TIP-20 credit to an owner with debt in that token, the credit repays creditors oldest first, each with a TIP-20 `Transfer` and `DebtRepaid`. A creditor the payment check refuses is skipped and keeps its debt; the credit never reverts for it. To skip a read on every credit, the TIP-403 registry counts per account the tokens in which it owes debt, in spare bytes of its receive-policy slot; a zone, which mirrors the registry from L1, counts them in the core's own storage. Funds a release frees are not credits.

## TIP-20 integration

- Reserving and unreserving go through TIP-20 internals that settle the owner's rewards first; reserved funds leave the opted-in supply and earn no rewards.
- Every debit reads only the balance slot, which excludes reserved funds; `balanceOf` adds `reserved`. Fee affordability checks use the balance slot. A credit re-reads the recipient's warm receive-policy slot (100 gas); only recipients with debt reach the core.

## Activation

The precompile, the TIP-20 hooks and the TIP-403 debt count activate at T16, on Tempo L1 and on zones in the same fork. Before T16 nothing changes. At the T16 boundary the core's account gets its native-precompile marker; both block executors apply this through one shared T16 step, so L1 and zones make it at the same boundary and never earlier.

## Zones

A zone runs the same core over its private balances:

- **Account.** From the T16 boundary, as on L1, the core's account gets its native-precompile marker, so its storage is never cleared as an empty account's.
- **Writes.** Every state-changing call needs T16 and the portal admin's transfer opt-in (`transfersEnabled`), as a zone-internal TIP-20 transfer does (`Unauthorized` otherwise).
- **Reads.** A view about an owner answers only the owner and the counterparties it chose: `getHold` the owner, placer and payee (anyone else reads an empty record); `reserved` and `available` the owner and a holder with a budget or outstanding holds (strangers read zero); `debtOf` the owner, its creditors and its holders; `budget` its two parties; `payeeTerms` the owner and payee. Every other view reverts `Unauthorized`. `releaseExpired`, which anyone may call, refuses a caller other than the owner, placer or payee with `HoldNotFound` until the hold expires, as for a missing hold, so it reveals no live hold.
- **Settlement.** A capture pays the payee inside the zone; issuers withdraw their settlement balance to L1 through the zone outbox once per cycle.

## Placer obligations

Deleting a settled record is what keeps a hold at two slots (three when it is the owner's only live record in the token, counting the position slot) and lets later records reuse the storage credits it leaves (about 62,000 gas for a steady-state hold against about 552,000 with permanent slots). The core therefore keeps nothing about a record once it settles, and every placer that needs more must keep it in its own storage:

- **Replay protection.** An id is free again once its record settles, and `hold` with that id then places a new record. A placer that must never act twice on one purchase (an authorization id, a mandate payment) keeps its own used-id record, or derives each id from state it never repeats, such as a nonce, and rejects reuse before calling the core.
- **Link to the original purchase.** The core forgets the token, payee and amounts of a settled record. A placer that refunds, disputes or reports against a purchase stores that link itself, from its own calls or from `HoldCaptured` and `HoldReleased`, which carry owner, placer, id, token, payee and amounts.
- **Cumulative refund caps.** The core's `refund` is not bounded by what was captured, and cannot be after deletion. It moves only the caller's own balance to the owner, under the payment check, and forgives only what the owner owes the caller. A refund over the captured amount takes nothing from the owner or anyone else; it is a payment the caller chose to make. A placer that pays refunds on behalf of others (a card program refunding from its settlement balance) must enforce `refunded <= captured` per purchase in its own storage before paying.

To keep any of this in the core instead, a record would keep a third slot (cumulative captured and refunded, and an occupied marker) after it settles. That costs one permanent new slot, 245,000 state gas, for every purchase, and would be a new hardfork.

## Product constraints

These are deliberate limits of the core. Each is consensus code, so changing one means a hardfork.

- **At most 4 creditors per (owner, token).** Every credit to an owner with debt walks its creditors, so the number bounds the work, and the gas, a plain transfer can add. A capture or charge that would need debt to a fifth creditor reverts `TooManyCreditors`; a capture paid fully from its hold never needs debt and is unaffected. A higher number raises the worst-case cost of every credit to a debtor by one slot read and write per creditor. It is `MAX_CREDITORS`.
- **Debt lapses on-chain after at most 365 days.** A lapsed debt is dropped from the core: incoming credits stop repaying it and it no longer counts the owner as a debtor (`DebtLapsed` records the amount). This is only an on-chain repayment window. It does not discharge, forgive or settle the debt between owner and creditor, and the creditor may still pursue it by other means. Each owner sets the term per creditor (`debtTerm`, 0 for the maximum); the maximum is `MAX_DEBT_TERM`. The bound exists so that a creditor cannot hold a claim on all of an owner's future income indefinitely.
- **Compliance exemptions.** Two payments skip only the sender's TIP-403 authorization, and nothing else is exempt. (a) A capture paid entirely from what an ordinary hold still backs pays its own payee after the owner is blocked, because the funds were reserved for that payee while the owner was authorized; the window is at most the hold's 31 days, and the placer, the payee or a recipient block ends it sooner. (b) A lien seizure pays its `LIEN_ROLE` authority whatever the owner's status, because a lien is an issuer freeze. Pause, recipient authorization and receive policies apply to both. Compliance burns of a blocked owner may take what holds reserve, recorded as a shortfall that ending holds absorb first; no new hold reserves funds until it is absorbed, so the exemption pays only funds reserved before the burn, and pledges and liens stay fully backed. `burnAt` of an owner in good standing never touches reserved funds. Removing exemption (a) would mean a blocked cardholder's settled purchases fail to pay merchants who already delivered.
- **No default or liquidation.** The core never seizes reserved or pledged funds for debt and has no notion of default. Debt is repaid only from the owner's incoming credits of that token, oldest creditor first, skipping a creditor the payment check refuses, until it is paid or lapses. Funds a release frees are not credits and repay nothing. Collateral is taken only by its own authority: a pledge or lien is seized by calling `capture` on it, paying only what it still backs and never creating debt. Any default process (notices, collections, liquidating a pledge on a missed payment) belongs to the holder contract or the creditor, using these primitives.

## Gas and storage

Storage per (owner, token): a position slot (`reserved` u128, creditor count, shortfall flag, the part pledges and liens hold as u64), a shortfall slot written only after compliance burns, and one slot per creditor. Per (owner, holder, token): one budget slot. Per (owner, payee, token): one terms slot. Per record: two slots, deleted on settlement.

New core slots per operation, enforced by `test_slots_per_operation` (`tempo-precompiles`):

| Operation | New core slots |
| --- | --- |
| `hold`, `holdFrom`, `pledge`, `pledgeFrom`, `lien` | 2, plus 1 for the position slot when the owner had nothing reserved or owed in the token |
| `capture` part, `adjust` | 0 |
| `capture` final, `release`, `releaseExpired`, seizing a pledge or lien to zero | −2 (and −1 more when the owner's position empties) |
| `charge`, `refund` | 0, plus 1 for a new creditor's debt |
| `approveHolder`, `setPayeeTerms` | 1 the first time |

Transaction gas, enforced against per-operation budgets and snapshotted by `test_reservation_core_gas` (`tempo-evm`, T16, legacy transactions, intrinsic gas included). A new slot costs 245,000 state gas (TIP-1016); each slot a settlement deletes mints a TIP-1060 storage credit on the core's account, which a later record consumes instead, so in steady state, with records settling as fast as they are placed, a hold costs about 62,000 gas. An owner who keeps one hold at a time, as most cardholders do, writes and deletes the position slot with every hold, so each of its holds takes three slots, all three from storage credits in steady state (`test_slots_single_hold_cycle`):

## Interface

```solidity
interface IReservationCore {
    struct HoldRequest {
        address token;
        bytes32 id;
        uint128 amount;
        uint64 expiresAt;
        address payee;
        bool standIn;
    }

    struct HoldRecord {
        address token;
        address payee;
        uint128 held;
        uint128 captureLimit;
        uint64 expiresAt;
        uint8 kind; // 0 none, 1 hold, 2 pledge, 3 lien
        bool standIn;
    }

    struct Budget {
        uint128 limit;
        uint128 spent;
        uint128 outstanding;
        uint32 period;
        uint64 periodStart;
    }

    struct PayeeTerms {
        uint128 debtCap;
        uint128 offlineCap;
        uint16 toleranceBps;
        uint32 debtTerm;
    }

    struct Debt {
        address creditor;
        uint128 amount;
        uint64 lapsesAt;
    }

    function approveHolder(address holder, address token, uint128 limit, uint32 period) external;
    function setPayeeTerms(address payee, address token, PayeeTerms calldata terms) external;

    function hold(HoldRequest calldata request) external;
    function holdFrom(address owner, HoldRequest calldata request) external;
    function pledge(address authority, address token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint64 expiresAt) external;
    function pledgeFrom(address owner, address token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint64 expiresAt) external;
    function lien(address owner, address token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint64 expiresAt) external;

    function adjust(address owner, address placer, bytes32 id, uint128 amount) external;
    function capture(address owner, address placer, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, bool isFinal) external;
    function release(address owner, address placer, bytes32 id) external;
    function releaseExpired(address owner, address placer, bytes32 id) external;
    function charge(address owner, address token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, address payee, bool standIn) external;
    function refund(address owner, address token, address placer, bytes32 id, uint128 amount) external;
    function clearLapsedDebt(address owner, address token) external;

    function getHold(address owner, address placer, bytes32 id) external view returns (HoldRecord memory);
    function reserved(address owner, address token) external view returns (uint128);
    function available(address owner, address token) external view returns (uint128);
    function budget(address owner, address holder, address token) external view returns (Budget memory);
    function payeeTerms(address owner, address payee, address token) external view returns (PayeeTerms memory);
    function debtOf(address owner, address token) external view returns (Debt[] memory);

    event HolderApproved(address indexed owner, address indexed holder, address indexed token, uint128 limit, uint32 period);
    event PayeeTermsSet(address indexed owner, address indexed payee, address indexed token, PayeeTerms terms);
    event HoldPlaced(address indexed owner, address indexed placer, address indexed token, bytes32 id, uint8 kind, address payee, uint128 amount, uint128 held, uint128 captureLimit, uint64 expiresAt, bool standIn);
    event HoldAdjusted(address indexed owner, address indexed placer, address indexed token, bytes32 id, uint128 held, uint128 captureLimit);
    event HoldCaptured(address indexed owner, address indexed placer, address indexed token, bytes32 id, address payee, uint128 amount, uint128 debt, bool isFinal);
    event HoldReleased(address indexed owner, address indexed placer, address indexed token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, bool expired);
    event Charged(address indexed owner, address indexed charger, address indexed token, bytes32 id, address payee, uint128 amount, uint128 debt);
    event Refunded(address indexed owner, address indexed payee, address indexed token, address placer, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint128 forgiven);
    event DebtRepaid(address indexed owner, address indexed creditor, address indexed token, uint128 amount);
    event DebtLapsed(address indexed owner, address indexed creditor, address indexed token, uint128 amount);

    error HoldExists();
    error HoldNotFound();
    error HoldExpired();
    error HoldNotExpired();
    error InsufficientAvailable();
    error BudgetExceeded();
    error CaptureLimitExceeded();
    error DebtCapExceeded();
    error TooManyCreditors();
    error PolicyForbids();
    error Unauthorized();
    error InvalidAmount();
    error InvalidExpiry();
}
```

# Compatibility

Before T16 nothing changes. From T16, accounts that never use the core behave as before except for about 100 gas per TIP-20 credit and one more read in `balanceOf`. Wallets should show `available` as spendable; holds emit no `Transfer`, and payments and repayments do. The transaction pool re-checks a fee payer's pending transactions when `HoldPlaced` or `HoldAdjusted` names it as owner (topic 1) with the token (topic 3), since only those lower a balance slot without a `Transfer`.

# Observability

Every state change emits one event. Every record event indexes owner, placer and token, so indexers rebuild live records from events; debt events index owner, creditor and token.

# Threat Model

| Actor | Controls | Bound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Holder | `holdFrom`, `adjust`, `capture`, `release`, `charge`, `pledgeFrom` for owners that budgeted it | `spent + outstanding <= limit`, with `spent` never returned within a period; captures within each hold's limit; debt within the owner's caps and terms; holds end within 31 days and pledges within 400 |
| Owner access key | `hold`, `adjust`, `capture`, `release`, `charge` on its own account | TIP-1011 limit, charged each hold's full capture limit and each charge; cannot change budgets, terms or pledge |
| Owner root key | budgets, payee terms, pledges | Already controls all funds |
| Payee | `capture`, `release` of holds to it; `refund` | Captures within each hold's limit and debt within the owner's caps |
| Pledge authority | reduce, release, seize of its pledges | What was pledged, under the payment check, until expiry |
| Lien authority | `lien`, reduce, release, seize | `LIEN_ROLE` at placement and seizure, root key; seizure pays only itself |
| Other placers and strangers | nothing on records they did not place or receive | Ids are per placer; every write checks its caller |
| Anyone | `releaseExpired`, `clearLapsedDebt` | Expiry and debt term |

# Invariants

Checked after every step of random sequences across two owners, two holders (lifetime and periodic budgets), two payees with different terms, two tokens, a lien authority and a stranger, covering every write, transfers, policy changes and time (`proptest_random_sequences_match_the_model`). Each call's result is compared with a plain model, including the exact error:

1. For every (account, token): balance slot + `reserved` = `balanceOf`, and the token's supply is unchanged by the core.
2. `reserved(owner, token)` = Σ live records' `held`, less the shortfall after a compliance burn, which never exceeds Σ live holds' `held`. Burns run as `burnBlocked` and as a bridge's `burnAt`, of blocked and unblocked owners.
3. Every record matches the model; a settled record leaves no storage, and the core's non-zero slots are exactly 2 per live record plus live positions, budgets, terms and debt entries.
4. A budget's `outstanding` = Σ its holder's live holds' and pledges' `held`, and `spent + outstanding <= limit` at every draw.
5. Each creditor's debt is within `max(debtCap, offlineCap)` for it, there are at most 4 creditors per (owner, token), no debt stands past its term, and the owner is counted as a debtor exactly while it owes in some token.
6. From `expiresAt` a record can only be released.
