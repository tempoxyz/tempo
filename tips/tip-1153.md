# TIP-1153: Reservation Core

## Abstract

A precompile at `0x5245535600000000000000000000000000000000` keeps only what must be atomic with TIP-20 balances: holds, captures, refunds, capped debt and liens. A hold moves part of an owner's balance out of its TIP-20 balance slot and reserves it for a payee until a hard expiry. A hold is placed by the owner or by a **holder**, a contract the owner approved for the token with an allowance that bounds what it has outstanding. Card records, limits, windows, mandates, multi-leg purchases, credit lines and savings live in such holder contracts, outside the protocol.

## Motivation

Card authorization needs funds set aside without moving them, a final amount that may differ from the authorization, and refunds tied to the original payment. Holding funds in place keeps custody with the owner and needs no issuer float. Everything else a card program does changes often and differs per program, so it belongs in upgradeable contracts; the protocol only bounds what such a contract can do to an owner's balance.

## Assumptions

- Holder contracts are trusted by the owner up to the allowance it approves. A buggy holder can commit at most that allowance at a time.
- Debt caps, offline caps and capture tolerances are set by the owner's root key; an access key cannot raise them.
- Hold ids are unique per owner. A holder should derive its ids from its own address and its own reference.

# Specification

## Records

A hold record of an owner under an id holds the token, the placer (the owner, or the holder that placed it), the payee, what it still reserves (`held`), its capture cap before tolerance (`approved`), the captured and refunded totals, its expiry, the stand-in flag and the settled flag. A settled record (after a final capture, a release, an expiry release or a charge) keeps its id forever: a new hold or charge with that id reverts `HoldExists`.

## Holds

- `hold(request)`: the caller is the owner. `holdFrom(owner, request)`: the caller is a holder with a non-zero allowance from `owner` for the token (`Unauthorized` otherwise), and `outstanding + amount` must not exceed the allowance (`AllowanceExceeded`).
- `now < expiresAt <= now + 31 days` (`InvalidExpiry`); `amount` is non-zero and fits 64 bits (`InvalidAmount`).
- The payee resolves through the address registry (TIP-1022) and must be a valid recipient other than the owner; the token must not be paused and its TIP-403 policy must authorize owner to payee (`PolicyForbids`).
- An ordinary hold reserves `amount` and reverts `InsufficientAvailable` above the available balance. A **stand-in** hold records `amount`, approved offline, as `approved` and reserves `min(amount, available)`.
- An owner-placed hold counts its reserved amount against the signing access key's spending limit (TIP-1011). A holder's hold counts against its allowance instead.

## Adjust, capture, release

- `adjust(owner, id, amount)`: placer only, live holds only. Sets what the hold still reserves. Raising it needs the increase available, counts against the allowance or key limit, and raises `approved` to `captured + amount` if higher; a stand-in hold cannot be raised past its approval (`InvalidAmount`).
- `capture(owner, id, amount, isFinal)`: placer or payee, live holds only. The captured total may reach `approved * (10000 + tolerance) / 10000`, where the tolerance is the owner's for that payee and token, by default 0 (`ToleranceExceeded`). The capture pays from the hold first; any excess comes from the available balance, then from debt to the payee. A final capture releases what the hold still reserves and settles it. For a holder's hold, the excess past the hold must fit the allowance once the hold's freed amount is returned.
- **Policy at capture.** The token must not be paused and its TIP-403 policy must let the payee receive (`PolicyForbids`). A capture paid entirely from the hold does not re-check the owner as sender: those funds were reserved under the policy in force when the hold was placed. Raising a hold with `adjust`, and a capture whose excess comes from the available balance or debt, move funds that were never reserved and check owner to payee as a new hold does.
- `release(owner, id)`: placer or payee; `releaseExpired(owner, id)`: anyone, once `now >= expiresAt` (`HoldNotExpired`). Both return what the hold reserves and settle it.
- **Hard expiry.** From `expiresAt`, `adjust` and `capture` revert `HoldExpired`; the hold can only be released. A late presentment is a `charge`.
- Captures and releases return the holder's freed amount to its allowance. Revoking a holder (allowance 0) stops new holds and charges; its existing holds still capture, release and refund.

## Charge and refund

- `charge(owner, token, id, amount, payee, standIn)`: the owner, or a holder within its allowance (the charge alone must fit what is not outstanding; it leaves nothing outstanding). Pays from the available balance, then debt, and records a settled id with `captured = approved = amount`.
- `refund(owner, id, amount)`: the record's payee only, up to `captured - refunded` (`RefundExceedsCapture`), on live or settled records, under the token's pause and TIP-403 rules from payee to owner.

## Debt

`setDebtCap(creditor, token, cap)` bounds what the owner may owe one creditor in a token; `setOfflineCap(payee, token, cap)` raises that bound to `max(debtCap, offlineCap)` for stand-in holds and stand-in charges. Debt has one creditor per owner and token at a time; a payment that would owe a second creditor reverts `DebtCapExceeded`, as does one past the cap (or `InsufficientAvailable` with no cap). From T15, after every TIP-20 credit to an owner with debt in that token, `min(debt, amount)` moves to the creditor with a TIP-20 `Transfer` and the precompile emits `DebtRepaid`, unless the token is paused or its policy or the creditor's receive policy forbids it, in which case the debt waits for a later credit. To skip a read on every credit, the TIP-403 registry counts per account the tokens in which it owes debt, in spare bytes of its receive-policy slot (`reservationDebts`). Funds a release frees are not credits.

## Liens

- `lien(owner, token, id, amount, expiresAt)`: a holder of the token's `LIEN_ROLE` (`keccak256("LIEN_ROLE")`), signing with its root key. `expiresAt` 0 means none.
- `pledge(authority, token, id, amount, expiresAt)`: the owner's root key, for an authority it names; `expiresAt` is required.
- Both reserve up to the owner's available balance (`InsufficientAvailable`). Lien ids are per authority (`LienExists`).
- Only the authority calls `releaseLien`, `reduceLien` (ending the lien at or past its amount) and `seizeLien` (root key; before expiry; up to the lien's amount), which pays the authority itself and nowhere else. Seizing a role lien also needs the role. Anyone calls `releaseExpiredLien` once an expiring lien has expired (`LienNotExpired`).
- Captures, charges and debt never take liened funds.

## TIP-20 integration (T15)

- Reserving and unreserving go through TIP-20 internals (`TIP20Token::reserve`, `unreserve`) that settle the owner's rewards first. Reserved funds leave the opted-in supply and earn no rewards; they rejoin it when unreserved.
- Every debit reads only the balance slot, which excludes reserved funds, so it never spends them and needs no reservation read; `balanceOf` adds `reserved`. Fee affordability checks use the balance slot.
- Compliance burns (`burnBlocked`, `burnAt`) may take reserved funds. The shortfall is recorded as `unbacked`; ending holds and liens consumes it first, so the reservation behind the rest stays in place, and a capture pays only what is available, the rest as capped debt.

## Activation

The precompile and TIP-20 hooks run from T15, on Tempo L1 and on zones.

## Zones

A zone runs the same core over its private TIP-20 balances, so a card program deployed in the zone holds, captures and refunds a cardholder's zone balance exactly as on L1. The differences are what a zone needs to stay private and to settle:

- **Account.** Before every T15 zone block the core's account gets its native-precompile marker if it has none, so its storage is never cleared as an empty account's.
- **Debtors.** A zone mirrors the TIP-403 registry from L1 and cannot write it, so the core counts the tokens each owner owes debt in its own storage there.
- **Writes.** Every state-changing call needs T15 and the portal admin's transfer opt-in (`transfersEnabled`), as a zone-internal TIP-20 transfer does; otherwise it reverts `Unauthorized`.
- **Reads.** A view about an owner answers only the owner and the counterparties it chose: a hold's placer and payee (`getHold`), a holder the owner approved for the token or that still holds some (`reserved`, `available`, `liened`, `debtOf`, whose creditor may also read it), the two parties to an allowance, debt cap, capture tolerance or offline cap, and a lien's authority and owner. A stranger to a position reads zero from `reserved`, `available` and `liened`, since a program asks about every token in an owner's funding order, and a stranger to a hold reads an empty record from `getHold`, as for a hold that does not exist, since a program checks for a leg's hold before placing it; every other view reverts `Unauthorized`. Events stay inside the zone; its private RPC exposes only TIP-20 events, to their parties.
- **Programs.** Contract creation in a zone is limited to the protocol allowlist and to top-level creations by an active sequencer of the zone's portal, which deploys the card programs. Programs cannot create contracts.

### Settlement out of a zone

A capture pays the payee's balance inside the zone; nothing leaves the zone per purchase, so L1 never sees a purchase, its amount or its cardholder. A card program's payee is its issuer's settlement account. Once per settlement cycle the issuer withdraws that account's whole zone balance (its captures net of refunds paid back from it) to its own settlement account on L1 through the zone outbox's ordinary withdrawal, one withdrawal per issuer per cycle. L1 shows only the deposits that moved cardholders' money in and one amount per issuer per cycle. A batch L1 rejects delays the withdrawal and never undoes a capture: the zone's state is final in the zone, and the withdrawal processes once a valid batch carries it. A refund larger than what the settlement account still holds in the zone needs the issuer to deposit the rest first.

## Gas

Storage is packed to the slot budget. Per owner and token: a position slot (`reserved`, debt, shortfall flag) and a shortfall slot (`unbacked`, `liened`) written only by compliance burns and liens. A hold is a head slot (token, held, expiry) and a terms slot (payee, approved, flags), plus a placer slot for a holder's hold, plus a totals slot (captured, refunded) written on its first capture. A holder allowance is one slot (allowance, outstanding). A lien is two slots.

New slots the core writes, measured by `test_slots_per_operation`:

| --- | --- |

| `holdFrom` | 3 (plus 1 likewise) |

| `release`, `releaseExpired`, `refund` | 0 (ending the last hold clears the position slot) |

## Interface

```solidity
interface IReservationCore {
    struct HoldRequest { address token; bytes32 id; uint128 amount; uint64 expiresAt; address payee; bool standIn; }
    struct HoldRecord {
        address token; address placer; address payee; uint128 held; uint128 approved;
        uint128 captured; uint128 refunded; uint64 expiresAt; bool standIn; bool settled;
    }

    function approveHolder(address holder, address token, uint128 allowance) external;
    function setDebtCap(address creditor, address token, uint128 cap) external;
    function setCaptureTolerance(address payee, address token, uint32 toleranceBps) external;
    function setOfflineCap(address payee, address token, uint128 cap) external;

    function hold(HoldRequest calldata request) external;
    function holdFrom(address owner, HoldRequest calldata request) external;
    function adjust(address owner, bytes32 id, uint128 amount) external;
    function capture(address owner, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, bool isFinal) external;
    function release(address owner, bytes32 id) external;
    function releaseExpired(address owner, bytes32 id) external;
    function refund(address owner, bytes32 id, uint128 amount) external;
    function charge(address owner, address token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, address payee, bool standIn) external;

    function lien(address owner, address token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint64 expiresAt) external;
    function pledge(address authority, address token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint64 expiresAt) external;
    function releaseLien(bytes32 id) external;
    function reduceLien(bytes32 id, uint128 amount) external;
    function seizeLien(bytes32 id, uint128 amount) external;
    function releaseExpiredLien(address authority, bytes32 id) external;

    function getHold(address owner, bytes32 id) external view returns (HoldRecord memory);
    function reserved(address owner, address token) external view returns (uint128);
    function available(address owner, address token) external view returns (uint128);
    function allowance(address owner, address holder, address token) external view returns (uint128 allowance, uint128 outstanding);
    function debtOf(address owner, address token) external view returns (address creditor, uint128 amount);
    function debtCap(address owner, address creditor, address token) external view returns (uint128);
    function captureTolerance(address owner, address payee, address token) external view returns (uint32);
    function offlineCap(address owner, address payee, address token) external view returns (uint128);
    function lienOf(address authority, bytes32 id) external view returns (address owner, address token, uint128 amount, uint64 expiresAt, bool pledged);
    function liened(address owner, address token) external view returns (uint128);

    event HolderApproved(address indexed owner, address indexed holder, address indexed token, uint128 allowance);
    event HoldPlaced(address indexed owner, bytes32 indexed id, address indexed token, address placer, address payee, uint128 amount, uint128 held, uint64 expiresAt, bool standIn);
    event HoldAdjusted(address indexed owner, bytes32 indexed id, address indexed token, uint128 held);
    event HoldCaptured(address indexed owner, bytes32 indexed id, address indexed token, address payee, uint128 amount, uint128 debt, bool isFinal);
    event HoldReleased(address indexed owner, bytes32 indexed id, address indexed token, uint128 amount, bool expired);
    event Charged(address indexed owner, bytes32 indexed id, address indexed token, address payee, uint128 amount, uint128 debt);
    event Refunded(address indexed owner, bytes32 indexed id, address indexed token, address payee, uint128 amount);
    event DebtRepaid(address indexed owner, address indexed token, address indexed creditor, uint128 amount);
    event Liened(address indexed owner, address indexed authority, address indexed token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint64 expiresAt, bool pledged);
    event LienReleased(address indexed owner, address indexed authority, address indexed token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, bool expired);
    event LienReduced(address indexed owner, address indexed authority, address indexed token, bytes32 id, uint128 remaining);
    event LienSeized(address indexed owner, address indexed authority, address indexed token, bytes32 id, uint128 amount, uint128 remaining);

    error HoldExists(); error HoldNotFound(); error HoldExpired(); error HoldNotExpired(); error HoldSettled();
    error InsufficientAvailable(); error AllowanceExceeded(); error ToleranceExceeded(); error RefundExceedsCapture();
    error DebtCapExceeded(); error PolicyForbids(); error Unauthorized(); error InvalidAmount(); error InvalidExpiry();
    error LienExists(); error LienNotFound(); error LienNotExpired();
}
```

# Compatibility

Before T15 nothing changes. From T15, accounts that never use the core behave as before except for about 100 gas per TIP-20 credit and one more read in `balanceOf`. Wallets should show `available` as spendable; holds emit no `Transfer`, and repayments do. The transaction pool re-checks a fee payer's pending transactions when any core event except `DebtRepaid` names it as owner (topic 1) with the token (topic 3).

# Observability

Every state change emits one event above. Hold and lien events index the owner and token, so the pool and indexers rebuild positions and settlement from events alone.

# Threat Model

| Actor | Controls | Bound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Holder contract | holdFrom, adjust, capture, release, charge for owners that approved it | Outstanding holds plus any single charge within the allowance, captures within approval and tolerance, debt within the root-key caps; revocation stops new holds |
| Owner access key | hold, adjust, capture, release, charge on its own account | TIP-1011 scope and spending limit; cannot change caps, tolerances or approvals |
| Owner root key | approvals, caps, tolerances, pledges | Already controls all funds |
| Payee | capture, release, refund of its records | Captures within approval and tolerance; refunds within what it captured |
| Lien authority | lien, reduce, release, seize in one token | `LIEN_ROLE` and root key; seizure pays only itself |
| Pledge authority | reduce, release, seize of pledges to it | What the owner pledged, until expiry |
| Anyone | releaseExpired, releaseExpiredLien | Expiry |

**Policy changes after authorization.** A hold is the owner's authorization, checked against the token's TIP-403 policy when it is placed. If the owner is blocked afterwards, its live holds can still be captured to a payee the policy lets receive, within what each hold reserves, so a merchant that delivered against an authorization is paid; the owner cannot use this to move more, because new holds, increases, captures past the hold and charges from a blocked owner revert `PolicyForbids`, and nothing reserved can reach a payee the policy blocks. To stop such funds, the placer or payee releases the hold, or the policy blocks the payee. A hold lasts at most 31 days, which bounds how long funds authorized before a block stay payable.

# Invariants

Tested as properties over random sequences of hold, holdFrom, adjust, capture, release, releaseExpired, refund, charge, lien, transfer and time steps (`proptest_random_sequences_keep_the_invariants`):

1. For every (owner, token): balance slot + `reserved` = `balanceOf`, and the token's total supply = Σ balance slots + Σ `reserved`.
2. `reserved(owner, token)` = Σ live holds' `held` + Σ live liens' amounts, less `unbacked` after a compliance burn.
3. Per hold: `captured <= approved * (1 + tolerance)`; `refunded <= captured`; `held = 0` once settled; a settled id never reopens.
4. `outstanding(owner, holder, token)` <= the allowance when each hold or increase was placed, and = Σ that holder's live holds' `held`.
5. `debt(owner, token) <= max(debtCap, offlineCap)` for its creditor, with one creditor at a time.
6. From `expiresAt`, a hold can only be released.
