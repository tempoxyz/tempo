# TIP-1126: TIP-20 Asset Token Extensions

## Abstract

This TIP lets issuers of tokenized stocks and funds issue them as ordinary TIP-20 tokens. It adds to every TIP-20 token a choice of 6 or 18 decimals, partial freezes and forced transfers (ERC-7943), a UI multiplier for splits (ERC-8056), renaming, and issuer metadata such as an ISIN.

This TIP also defines five corporate actions on Tempo and in zones: renames, splits, reinvested dividends, cash dividends, and mergers. A rename, split, or reinvested dividend changes one value on Tempo, and zones read that value. A cash dividend or merger treats each zone as one holder on Tempo: the issuer pays the zone's share with one call to its portal, and the zone credits each of its holders.

Actions where each holder chooses what to receive are out of scope. A token that never uses these features behaves as it does today.

## Motivation

Issuers of tokenized assets need controls that TIP-20 does not have:

- **Decimals.** TIP-20 is fixed at 6 decimals. Many asset tokens and bridged assets use 18. Converting them to 6 loses precision in bridges and trading.
- **Freezes, seizures, and lost keys.** Transfer agents must hold or move specific amounts under court orders and sanctions. They must also recover holdings when a holder loses a key. ERC-7943 standardizes these controls.
- **Corporate actions.** Issuers rename tokens, split shares, pay dividends, and retire shares when companies merge. TIP-20 cannot rename a token, and has no standard way to record a split.
- **Security identifiers.** Integrators need to map a token to the security it represents, for example by ISIN.

Zones let holders keep and settle positions privately. A zone runs no issuer contracts, and its balances are private. An issuer therefore cannot see whom to pay in a zone, and cannot run its usual dividend or merger process there. Without a defined path, zone holders would miss corporate actions.

### Alternatives Considered

1. **A separate token type at its own address prefix.** An earlier draft of this TIP. A second prefix duplicates routing, recipient validation, keychain rules, and zone enablement. Existing issuers would also lose their token address to adopt it.
2. **A separate prefix only for 18-decimal tokens.** Decimals affect no transfer path. Only the fee manager and the stablecoin DEX assume 6 decimals, and both accept only `USD` tokens. Forbidding `USD` with 18 decimals protects them without a second prefix.
3. **Rebasing balances for splits.** Rebasing changes `balanceOf` whenever the share count changes, which breaks DeFi accounting. ERC-8056 keeps balances fixed and changes only how they are displayed.
4. **A dividend accumulator on every Tempo token.** Each transfer would settle per-holder dividend state, as TIP-20 rewards did. TIP-1075 removed rewards because of that cost on the payment path. This TIP keeps Tempo transfers unchanged and settles per holder only inside zones.
5. **Wrapping assets before they enter a zone.** A wrapper contract would hold the asset on Tempo and pay dividends when holders leave the zone. Holders would wait until exit to receive cash. The wrapper would need its own compliance controls, and integrators would see two tokens per asset.
6. **A token-level hook that calls issuer code on every transfer.** Precompiles cannot call EVM code today, and zones cannot run arbitrary contracts. See [Extension Points](#extension-points-non-normative).

## Assumptions

| Assumption | Consequence if violated |
|---|---|
| The TIP-20 implementation at activation includes TIP-1006 (`burnAt`), TIP-1028 (receive policies), and TIP-1075 (rewards deprecated). | The frozen-token rules for `burnAt` and receive-policy redirects refer to behavior that does not exist. |
| The fee manager and stablecoin DEX accept a token only if its `currency()` is `USD`, and they are the only components that assume 6 decimals. | An 18-decimal token could reach a component that misprices it. That component needs its own decimals check. |
| Zones read selected Tempo storage at their Tempo anchor and reject zone writes to it. Zones do this today for the TIP-403 registry and, from T15, the TIP-20 pause flag. | Token state could differ between Tempo and a zone. The [zone rules](#zones) would need a different delivery path. |
| A zone processes its portal's queue entries in order, before any zone transaction that follows them. | The record point of a distribution would not be well defined. |
| ERC-8056 is a Draft. This TIP follows its text and reference implementation at ERCs commit `554d3467b29742aae0f864cdd2fdb492510a91ba` (2026-09-01). | Later changes to ERC-8056 do not apply unless a TIP adopts them. |

# Specification

## Overview

### Features

Every TIP-20 token gains these features at activation:

| Feature | What the issuer can do | Standard | Gated by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decimals | Choose 6 or 18 at creation. Immutable. | — | Set at creation |
| Partial freeze | Hold a chosen amount of an account's tokens. The rest moves normally. | ERC-7943 | `FREEZE_ROLE` |
| Forced transfer | Move tokens without the holder's authorization, for seizures and lost-key recovery. | ERC-7943 | `FORCED_TRANSFER_ROLE` |
| UI multiplier | Record splits and reinvested dividends in how balances are displayed. No balance changes. | ERC-8056 | `CORPORATE_ACTION_ROLE` |
| Name and symbol | Update `name` and `symbol` after creation. | — | `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` |
| Metadata | Publish a contract URI and key-value entries such as an ISIN. | ERC-7572 (URI) | `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` |

Nobody holds a new role until the admin grants it. Existing tokens read as 6 decimals, a UI multiplier of `1e18`, nothing frozen, and empty metadata.

### Definitions

```
frozen(a)   = frozen amount of a
unfrozen(a) = balanceOf(a) - frozen(a)
```

- **Balance.** `balanceOf(a)` is the holder's total balance, frozen and unfrozen together.
- **Protected address.** An address `burnAt` refuses: the TIP-20 `PROTECTED` system custody addresses active at the current hardfork (including `ReceivePolicyGuard`), the token's own address, and ZonePortal addresses.
- **Portal.** The ZonePortal contract on Tempo that holds every token deposited into a zone. On Tempo, a portal is one holder of each token.
- **Asset.** The token a corporate action applies to.
- **Payout token.** The token holders receive from a distribution: a stablecoin for a cash dividend, or the acquirer's token for a merger.
- **Distribution.** A payment of a fixed amount of a payout token per unit of an asset, to every zone holder of the asset.
- **Record point.** The moment a zone applies a distribution. Holders are paid on their balances at that moment.
- **In-flight withdrawal.** A withdrawal the zone has processed but Tempo has not yet released from the portal.

### Corporate Actions

| Action | On Tempo | In a zone |
|---|---|---|
| Rename or ticker change | `setName`, `setSymbol` | The zone reads the new name and symbol. |
| Split or reverse split | `setUIMultiplier` | The zone reads the new UI multiplier. |
| Dividend reinvested in shares | `setUIMultiplier` | The zone reads the new UI multiplier. |
| Cash dividend | The issuer snapshots holders and pays each one. | The issuer calls `distribute` on the portal. The zone credits each holder. |
| Merger, acquisition, or delisting | The issuer burns the asset with `burnAt` and pays holders in the payout token. | The issuer calls `distribute` with `cancelAsset`. The zone credits each holder and cancels the asset. |

The first three are **token-level** actions: they change one value that applies to every holder. The last two are **holder-level** actions: each holder receives an amount proportional to their balance.

Out of scope: elective actions, where each holder chooses what to receive, such as cash-or-stock elections, tender offers, and rights offerings (see [Open Questions](#open-questions)).

### How Zones Fit

- **Token-level state** (name, symbol, decimals, UI multiplier, metadata, pause flag, transfer policy) comes from Tempo. The issuer changes it once, on Tempo, and every zone reads it.
- **Holder-level actions** go through the portal. The issuer pays the portal's share with `distribute`. The zone splits that payment among its holders.
- **Freezes and forced transfers** apply to Tempo balances only. To act on one holder inside a zone, the issuer blocks the address in the transfer policy, or calls `forceWithdraw` on the portal.

### Example

An issuer creates an 18-decimal stock token with currency `""` and an allowlist transfer policy. It grants `FREEZE_ROLE` and `FORCED_TRANSFER_ROLE` to its compliance contract, and `CORPORATE_ACTION_ROLE` to its corporate-actions desk. Alice holds 100 tokens on Tempo. Bob holds 40 tokens in a zone, whose portal holds 1,000 tokens in total.

1. **Split.** The company splits 4-for-1. The issuer schedules the UI multiplier to change from `1e18` to `4e18`. After it takes effect, Alice's `balanceOf` is still 100 and her `balanceOfUI` is 400. The zone reads the same multiplier, so Bob's wallet shows 160.
2. **Cash dividend.** The company pays $0.25 per share. Each token now represents 4 shares, so the dividend is $1.00 per token. The issuer pauses the token and calls `distribute` on the portal at $1.00 per token. The portal pulls $1,000. The issuer snapshots Tempo holders, unpauses, and pays Alice $100. Inside the zone, Bob can claim $40.
3. **Freeze and seizure.** A court freezes 30 of Alice's 100 tokens. Alice can still transfer up to 70. The court then orders 80 tokens moved. `forcedTransfer` takes Alice's 70 unfrozen tokens and 10 frozen ones. Alice is left with 20 tokens, all frozen.
4. **Merger.** An acquirer buys the company at 0.5 acquirer shares per share, which is 2 acquirer tokens per token. The issuer calls `distribute` on the portal with the acquirer's token, a rate of 2, and `cancelAsset` set. Inside the zone, Bob's 40 tokens are cancelled and Bob can claim 80 acquirer tokens. On Tempo, the issuer burns Alice's 20 tokens with `burnAt` and mints Alice 40 acquirer tokens.
5. **Rename.** The surviving company changes its ticker. The admin calls `setSymbol`. Wallets on Tempo and in the zone show the new symbol.

## Activation

All behavior in this TIP applies from the protocol version assigned to it, to every TIP-20 token, portal, and zone. Before activation, the new functions follow unknown-selector behavior.

## Token Creation

`TIP20Factory` adds a `createToken` overload with two more arguments, `decimals` and `transferPolicyId`. It validates every argument before writing any state:

1. Revert `InvalidDecimals` unless `decimals` is 6 or 18.
2. Revert `InvalidCurrency` if `decimals` is 18 and `currency` is `USD`.
3. Revert `InvalidTransferPolicyId` unless `transferPolicyId` exists in the TIP-403 registry. The built-in policies `0` (reject all) and `1` (allow all) exist.
4. Apply every check of the existing `createToken` overload with `logoURI`.
5. Create the token as that overload does, store `decimals`, and bind `transferPolicyId` to the token.
6. Emit the existing overload's events, then `TokenCreatedWithDecimals(token, decimals, transferPolicyId)`.

Binding the policy at creation means a regulated token is never unrestricted, even before its first mint. Tokens created with the existing overloads have 6 decimals and policy `1`.

`decimals()` returns 6 or 18 and never changes after creation. Amounts in every function and event are in raw units.

Asset tokens SHOULD use a `currency` other than `USD`, which keeps them out of the fee manager and stablecoin DEX. Asset tokens SHOULD record the underlying asset's denomination in [extra metadata](#extra-metadata).

## Name and Symbol

`setName(newName)` and `setSymbol(newSymbol)`:

1. Revert `Unauthorized` unless the caller holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`.
2. Apply the validation `createToken` applies to `name` or `symbol`.
3. Store the new value.
4. Emit `NameUpdated(msg.sender, newName)` or `SymbolUpdated(msg.sender, newSymbol)`.

Both work while the token is paused.

The EIP-712 domain separator used by `permit` includes `name`. A rename therefore changes `DOMAIN_SEPARATOR()`, and permits signed before the rename fail after it. Nonces are unchanged.

## Frozen Tokens

An account with `FREEZE_ROLE` sets a frozen amount for any account. The holder cannot move the frozen amount. The rest of the balance moves normally.

### `setFrozenTokens(account, amount)`

1. Revert `Unauthorized` unless the caller holds `FREEZE_ROLE`.
2. Revert `ProtectedAddress` if `account` is protected.
3. Revert `VirtualAddressNotAllowed` if `account` is a TIP-1022 virtual address. Virtual addresses hold no balance; their master does.
4. Revert `InsufficientBalance` if `amount > balanceOf(account)`.
5. Set `frozen(account) = amount`.
6. Emit `Frozen(account, amount)`. Return `true`.

`setFrozenTokens` works while the token is paused. To hold tokens that have not arrived yet, the issuer freezes them after they arrive, or blocks the account in the transfer policy.

### Effect on Each Operation

| Operation | Debited account | Limited to `unfrozen`? | Changes `frozen`? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `transfer`, `transferWithMemo` | caller | Yes | No |
| `transferFrom`, `transferFromWithMemo` | `from` | Yes | No |
| `burn`, `burnWithMemo` | caller | Yes | No |
| Deposit into a zone | depositor | Yes | No |
| Receive-policy redirect of any of the above (TIP-1028) | sender | Yes | No |
| Fee payment through the fee manager | payer | Yes | No |
| `burnAt`, `burnBlocked` | burned account | No | Yes |
| `forcedTransfer` | `from` | No | Yes |
| `mint`, incoming transfers | none | n/a | No |
| `approve`, `permit` | none | n/a | No |

**Limited debits.** A limited debit of `amount` from `a` reverts if `amount > unfrozen(a)`. The error is `ERC7943InsufficientUnfrozenBalance(a, amount, unfrozen(a))` if `amount <= balanceOf(a)`, and `InsufficientBalance` otherwise. The check runs before the TIP-1028 receive-policy check, so it also limits a transfer that would be redirected to `ReceivePolicyGuard`.

**Privileged debits.** Let `u = unfrozen(a)` before the debit. If `amount > u`, the token sets `frozen(a) = frozen(a) - (amount - u)` before the balance changes, and emits `Frozen(a, frozen(a))` before the `Transfer` event.

### Storage of Frozen Amounts

`balances[a]` holds the unfrozen amount, and a separate `frozen[a]` mapping holds the frozen amount. `balanceOf(a)` returns their sum. `setFrozenTokens` moves tokens between the two slots and leaves `balanceOf` and `totalSupply` unchanged.

Transfers, fee payment, and the transaction pool's fee-balance check read only `balances[a]`, as today. The frozen check therefore adds no storage read to the payment path, and the fee manager and transaction pool need no change. `frozen[a]` is read only by `balanceOf`, privileged debits, and the failure path of a limited debit, to choose the error.

## Forced Transfer

`forcedTransfer(from, to, amount)`:

1. Revert `Unauthorized` unless the caller holds `FORCED_TRANSFER_ROLE`.
2. Revert `ProtectedAddress` if `from` is protected.
3. Resolve `to` as TIP-20 transfers do. A TIP-1022 virtual address resolves to its master. Revert `InvalidRecipient` if the resolved target is zero or has the TIP-20 prefix. Revert `ProtectedAddress` if it is protected.
4. Revert `ERC7943CannotReceive(to)` if `canReceive(to)` is false.
5. Revert `PolicyForbids` if the resolved target's TIP-1028 receive policy rejects this token from `from`.
6. Revert `InsufficientBalance` if `amount > balanceOf(from)`.
7. Apply the [privileged-debit](#effect-on-each-operation) rule to `from`.
8. Debit `from` and credit the target. Emit `Transfer(from, to, amount)`, plus the TIP-1022 forwarding `Transfer` for a virtual `to`. Emit `ForcedTransfer(from, to, amount)`. Return `true`.

Step 5 reverts instead of redirecting to `ReceivePolicyGuard`, because the guard's recovery path could return seized tokens to `from`.

A forced transfer does not check the pause flag, the sender side of the transfer policy, allowances, or access-key spending limits. A lost-key recovery is a `forcedTransfer` of the full balance to the holder's new address.

## Permission Views

- `canSend(account)`: the token's TIP-403 transfer policy authorizes `account` as sender.
- `canReceive(account)`: `account` resolves as a transfer recipient (TIP-1022), and the policy authorizes the resolved address as recipient.
- `canTransfer(from, to, amount)`: the token is not paused, `canSend(from)` and `canReceive(to)` are true, and `amount <= unfrozen(from)`.

The views revert only when they run out of gas, and return `false` on any other internal error. They do not evaluate TIP-1028 receive policies, allowances, or access-key spending limits.

## UI Multiplier

The UI multiplier tells wallets how to display balances. It never changes balances, transfers, allowances, total supply, the supply cap, or any event amount.

The token stores the multiplier in effect now (`uiMultiplier`), a scheduled multiplier (`newUIMultiplier`), and the time the scheduled one takes effect (`effectiveAt`). A stored multiplier of 0 reads as `1e18`, so existing tokens need no migration.

```
UI_MULTIPLIER_ONE = 1e18
MAX_UI_MULTIPLIER = 1e36
```

### Views

These follow the ERC-8056 reference implementation:

- `uiMultiplier()`: `newUIMultiplier` if `block.timestamp >= effectiveAt`, else `uiMultiplier`.
- `newUIMultiplier()`, `effectiveAt()`: the stored values.
- `toUIAmount(raw) = raw * uiMultiplier() / 1e18`, rounded down.
- `fromUIAmount(ui) = ui * 1e18 / uiMultiplier()`, rounded down.
- `balanceOfUI(a) = toUIAmount(balanceOf(a))`. `totalSupplyUI() = toUIAmount(totalSupply())`.

`toUIAmount` and `fromUIAmount` revert on overflow.

### Scheduling an Update

`setUIMultiplier(newMultiplier, effectiveAtTimestamp)`:

1. Revert `Unauthorized` unless the caller holds `CORPORATE_ACTION_ROLE`.
2. Revert `InvalidUIMultiplier` unless `0 < newMultiplier <= MAX_UI_MULTIPLIER`.
3. Revert `InvalidEffectiveAt` unless `block.timestamp < effectiveAtTimestamp < 2^64`.
4. If no update is pending (`block.timestamp >= effectiveAt`): let `old = newUIMultiplier`. Set `uiMultiplier = old`, `newUIMultiplier = newMultiplier`, and `effectiveAt = effectiveAtTimestamp`. Emit `UIMultiplierUpdated(old, newMultiplier, effectiveAtTimestamp)`.
5. If an update is pending: emit `UIMultiplierUpdateCancelled(newUIMultiplier, effectiveAt)`. Set `newUIMultiplier = newMultiplier` and `effectiveAt = effectiveAtTimestamp`. Emit `UIMultiplierUpdated(uiMultiplier, newMultiplier, effectiveAtTimestamp)`.

`cancelUIMultiplierUpdate()`:

1. Revert `Unauthorized` unless the caller holds `CORPORATE_ACTION_ROLE`.
2. Revert `NoPendingUIMultiplierUpdate` unless `block.timestamp < effectiveAt`.
3. Emit `UIMultiplierUpdateCancelled(newUIMultiplier, effectiveAt)`. Set `newUIMultiplier = uiMultiplier` and `effectiveAt = 0`.

Both functions work while the token is paused. No event is emitted when a scheduled multiplier takes effect; indexers derive that moment from `effectiveAt`.

## Metadata

### Contract URI

`contractURI()` returns the stored string.

`setContractURI(newContractURI)`:

1. Revert `Unauthorized` unless the caller holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`.
2. Revert `ContractURITooLong` if `newContractURI` is longer than 1024 bytes.
3. Revert `InvalidContractURI` if `newContractURI` is non-empty and its scheme fails the TIP-1026 `logoURI` scheme rules.
4. Store the value. Emit `ContractURIUpdated()`, which has no parameters because ERC-7572 defines it that way.

### Extra Metadata

`extraMetadata(key)` returns the value stored under `key`, or the empty string.

`setExtraMetadata(key, newValue)`:

1. Revert `Unauthorized` unless the caller holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`.
2. Revert `InvalidExtraMetadataKey` if `key` is empty or longer than 32 bytes.
3. Revert `ExtraMetadataValueTooLong` if `newValue` is longer than 256 bytes.
4. Store `newValue` under `key`, or remove the entry if `newValue` is empty.
5. Emit `ExtraMetadataUpdated(msg.sender, key, newValue)`.

Keys are case-sensitive. Entries cannot be listed on-chain; indexers rebuild them from events. Issuers SHOULD use lowercase keys, SHOULD store a 12-character ISIN under `isin`, and SHOULD store the underlying asset's denomination under `denomination`.

## Roles

| Role | Gates |
|---|---|
| `FREEZE_ROLE` | `setFrozenTokens` |
| `FORCED_TRANSFER_ROLE` | `forcedTransfer`; `forceWithdraw` on a portal |
| `CORPORATE_ACTION_ROLE` | `setUIMultiplier`, `cancelUIMultiplierUpdate`; `distribute` on a portal |
| `BURN_AT_ROLE` (TIP-1006) | `burnAt`; also required for `distribute` with `cancelAsset` |

`DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` administers all of them. It also gates `setName`, `setSymbol`, `setContractURI`, and `setExtraMetadata`, as it gates `setLogoURI`.

Freezing and forced transfer are separate roles, so an issuer can grant the power to hold funds without the power to move them. A cancelling distribution destroys holders' tokens inside a zone, so it requires the `BURN_AT_ROLE` that authorizes destroying them on Tempo.

## Zones

### Token State Read from Tempo

A zone MUST read these fields of each enabled token from Tempo state at its Tempo anchor, as it reads the TIP-403 registry and the pause flag today:

- `name`, `symbol`, and `decimals`
- `uiMultiplier`, `newUIMultiplier`, and `effectiveAt`
- `logoURI`, `contractURI`, and every `extraMetadata` entry
- the pause flag and the transfer policy binding (unchanged from today)

Zone transactions MUST NOT write these fields. The zone MUST revert `setName`, `setSymbol`, `setUIMultiplier`, `cancelUIMultiplierUpdate`, `setContractURI`, `setExtraMetadata`, and `setLogoURI` for every caller.

A rename, split, or reinvested dividend reaches a zone when the zone imports the Tempo block that contains it. A scheduled multiplier takes effect in the zone when the zone's block timestamp reaches `effectiveAt`.

### Freezes and Forced Transfers

Frozen amounts belong to Tempo accounts. The zone does not read `frozen[a]`, and zone balances are never frozen. A frozen amount cannot enter a zone, because a deposit is a limited debit.

`forcedTransfer`, `burnAt`, and `burnBlocked` cannot reach zone balances, because the portal is a protected address. An issuer acts on one zone holder in one of two ways:

1. **Block the address.** The issuer adds the address to the token's TIP-403 policy. The zone enforces that policy, so the address can no longer send, receive, or withdraw the token in the zone.
2. **Force a withdrawal.** The issuer calls `forceWithdraw` on the portal, which moves the holder's zone balance to a Tempo address the issuer names.

`forceWithdraw(asset, account, amount, to)` on a portal:

1. Revert `Unauthorized` unless the caller holds `FORCED_TRANSFER_ROLE` on `asset`.
2. Revert `TokenNotEnabled` unless `asset` is enabled in the zone.
3. Revert `InvalidRecipient` if `to` is zero, has the TIP-20 prefix, or is protected.
4. Append a forced-withdrawal entry to the portal's deposit queue. Emit `ForcedWithdrawalQueued(id, asset, account, amount, to)`.

When the zone reaches the entry, it:

1. Lets `debited = min(amount, balance of account)`.
2. Debits `debited` from `account`, ignoring the zone's pause flag, transfer policy, and transfer opt-in.
3. If `debited > 0`, queues a withdrawal of `debited` to `to`.
4. Emits `ForcedWithdrawalApplied(id, asset, account, debited)`.

The amount released on Tempo shows the issuer whether `account` held at least `amount`. The issuer named `account`, so this reveals nothing about other holders.

### Distributions

The issuer pays a portal's share of a dividend or merger with `distribute` instead of a transfer. The zone splits that share among its holders at the record point.

`distribute(asset, payoutToken, rate, cancelAsset, refundRecipient)` on a portal:

1. Revert `Unauthorized` unless the caller holds `CORPORATE_ACTION_ROLE` on `asset`, and also `BURN_AT_ROLE` if `cancelAsset` is true.
2. Revert `TokenNotEnabled` unless `asset` is enabled in the zone, and unless `payoutToken` is enabled if `rate > 0`.
3. Revert `InvalidDistribution` if `payoutToken == asset`, if `rate == 0` and `cancelAsset` is false, or if `asset` is cancelled in this portal.
4. Revert `InvalidRecipient` if `refundRecipient` is zero, has the TIP-20 prefix, or is protected.
5. Revert `TooManyPayoutTokens` if `payoutToken` would be the fifth distinct payout token distributed for `asset` in this portal.
6. Let `held` be the portal's balance of `asset`. Pull `funding = ceil(rate * held / 1e18)` of `payoutToken` from the caller.
7. If `cancelAsset` is true, mark `asset` cancelled in this portal. Deposits of a cancelled asset revert `AssetCancelled`.
8. Append a distribution entry to the portal's deposit queue. Emit `DistributionQueued(id, asset, payoutToken, msg.sender, rate, funding, cancelAsset, refundRecipient)`.

`rate` is in payout-token base units per asset base unit, scaled by `1e18`. The issuer uses the same rate it pays holders on Tempo. `rate` is per raw token: the issuer converts a per-share amount by multiplying it by `uiMultiplier() / 1e18`. A delisting with no payout uses `rate == 0` with `cancelAsset`.

`distribute` checks the asset's roles, not the portal's depositor allowlist. It works while the asset is paused.

#### Applying a Distribution

The zone applies the entry when it reaches it in the deposit queue. Every deposit queued before the entry has been credited by then, and none queued after it.

At the record point the zone:

1. Reads `S`, the zone's total supply of `asset`.
2. Records the distribution. Each holder `h` is entitled to `floor(rate * balance(h) / 1e18)` of `payoutToken`, where `balance(h)` is `h`'s balance of `asset` at the record point.
3. Lets `refund = funding - ceil(rate * S / 1e18)`. If `refund > 0`, queues a withdrawal of `refund` of `payoutToken` to `refundRecipient`.
4. If `cancelAsset` is true: sets the zone's supply of `asset` to zero, treats every holder's balance of `asset` as zero, and reverts every later transfer or withdrawal of `asset` with `AssetCancelled`. Queues a withdrawal of `S` of `asset` to `refundRecipient`, for the issuer to burn on Tempo.
5. Emits `DistributionApplied(id, asset, payoutToken, rate, S, refund)`.

The refund covers the gap between the portal's balance and the zone's supply. That gap is in-flight withdrawals, plus any tokens sent to the portal outside a deposit. Owners of in-flight withdrawals receive the asset on Tempo after the issuer's snapshot, so the issuer pays them from the refund.

#### Settlement

The zone settles each holder when the holder next touches the asset, not at the record point:

- Before any change to `h`'s balance of `asset` (a transfer, a withdrawal, or a deposit credit), the zone MUST add `h`'s unsettled entitlements to `h`'s claimable amount of each payout token.
- `claimDistributions(asset)` settles the caller and mints the caller's claimable amount of every payout token for `asset`. For a cancelled asset, it also clears the caller's balance of `asset`. It emits `DistributionClaimed(account, asset, payoutToken, amount)` for each payout token with a non-zero amount.
- `pendingDistribution(asset, payoutToken, account)` returns `account`'s unclaimed amount, including unsettled entitlements. Like `balanceOf` in a zone, only `account` may read it.

The zone keeps one cumulative per-unit index for each pair of asset and payout token. Settling one holder costs a constant number of storage reads and writes per payout token, and an asset has at most four payout tokens per portal. A transfer settles both sender and recipient. The zone's fixed transfer gas charge MUST cover this worst case, so the gas charged reveals nothing about pending distributions.

The zone credits at most `ceil(rate * S / 1e18)` of the payout token for a distribution. That is exactly what the portal keeps after the refund.

## Corporate Action Procedures (Non-Normative)

The issuer runs its usual process for Tempo holders, and calls `distribute` once on each portal that holds the asset.

**Cash dividend.**

1. Pause the asset. Transfers stop on Tempo and in every zone, and deposits stop, so no holding moves between Tempo and a zone.
2. Call `distribute(asset, stablecoin, rate, false, issuer)` on every portal that holds the asset, in one block.
3. Snapshot Tempo holders as of that block. Skip portals and other protected addresses.
4. Unpause the asset.
5. Pay each Tempo holder `rate × balance / 1e18` with batched transfers, with a memo that identifies the dividend.
6. When each portal's refund arrives, pay the owners of the in-flight withdrawals it covers.

With the snapshot in the same block as the `distribute` calls, each holding is counted once: in the snapshot, in a zone's supply, or in a refund.

**Merger.**

1. Change the asset's transfer policy so that only the portals and the issuer can send the asset. Holders can no longer trade it on Tempo or in zones, and portals can still release it.
2. Call `distribute(asset, acquirerToken, rate, true, issuer)` on every portal that holds the asset, in one block.
3. Snapshot Tempo holders as of that block.
4. For each Tempo holder, `burnAt` their balance and mint `rate × balance / 1e18` of the acquirer's token.
5. Burn the asset each portal returns. Convert in-flight withdrawals as they arrive.

The asset stays unpaused, because `burnAt` requires an unpaused token.

**Rename, split, or reinvested dividend.** Call `setName`, `setSymbol`, or `setUIMultiplier` on Tempo. Zone holders need nothing else. To halt trading around a split, pause the asset, schedule the multiplier a short time ahead, and unpause after `effectiveAt`. Zones read both the pause flag and the multiplier, so they halt and resume with Tempo.

A reinvested dividend multiplies the UI multiplier by `1 + d / p`, where `d` is the dividend per share net of any withholding and `p` is the share price used for reinvestment. The token then tracks the stock's total return.

## Changes to Other Components

- **TIP-20 factory.** The new `createToken` overload and the `USD`/18-decimal rule.
- **TIP-20 precompile.** The functions, roles, and storage in this TIP.
- **ZonePortal.** `distribute`, `forceWithdraw`, `isAssetCancelled`, and two new deposit-queue entry types. Deposits of a cancelled asset revert.
- **Zone execution.** Reads the fields in [Token State Read from Tempo](#token-state-read-from-tempo), reverts their setters, applies the two new queue entries, and adds `claimDistributions` and `pendingDistribution`. The new per-account views (`balanceOfUI`, `getFrozenTokens`, `canTransfer`, `pendingDistribution`) follow the zone's existing privacy rule for per-account reads.
- **No change.** The fee manager and transaction pool, which read only `balances[a]`. Precompile routing, recipient validation, the TIP-403 registry, the account keychain, the stablecoin DEX, the channel reserve, and payment classification.

## Extension Points (Non-Normative)

Issuer-defined transfer logic belongs in a future TIP-403 policy type answered by a contract. A token adopts it with `changeTransferPolicyId`. `canSend`, `canReceive`, and `canTransfer` reflect it, because they delegate to TIP-403. A zone cannot run that contract, so a token with such a policy needs a simple allowlist or blocklist policy for zones to enforce alongside it.

Other holder-level actions that pay a fixed amount per share, such as spin-offs and special dividends, are distributions with a different payout token. They need no protocol change.

An issuer that wants limits on multiplier updates, such as a maximum change per update or a minimum time between updates, grants `CORPORATE_ACTION_ROLE` to a contract that enforces them. The same approach adds approval flows to `distribute`.

## Open Questions

1. **Elective actions.** Cash-or-stock elections, tender offers, and rights offerings let each holder choose. Distributions pay every holder the same way. Zone holders would withdraw to Tempo to elect.
2. **Withdrawals across a merger.** An in-flight withdrawal at a cancelling distribution delivers the old asset on Tempo, and the issuer converts it there. Alternatively, the portal could release the payout token instead.
3. **Settlement gas.** Zones charge a fixed 100,000 gas per transfer today. A transfer that settles two holders across four payout tokens may need more. The fixed charge may need to rise, or the payout-token limit to fall.
4. **Failed refunds.** A refund or cancelled-asset withdrawal to `refundRecipient` can fail on Tempo, for example if the transfer policy blocks it. Today a failed withdrawal bounces back to its sender in the zone, and a system withdrawal has no sender.
5. **Portal fees.** `distribute` and `forceWithdraw` add queue entries the zone must process. Whether they pay the portal's deposit fee, and in which token, is open.
6. **Payment lane.** TIP-1045 classifies asset-token transfers as payments, because classification is by address prefix. Excluding them would need a stateful check at classification time.

### Storage

Each TIP-20 token adds:

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `decimals` | `uint8` | 0 (reads as 6) or 18. Set at creation, immutable. |
| `frozen` | `mapping(address => uint256)` | Frozen amount per account. Default 0. `balances` holds the unfrozen amount. |
| `uiMultiplier` | `uint256` | Multiplier in effect before `effectiveAt`. 0 reads as `1e18`. |
| `newUIMultiplier` | `uint256` | Multiplier in effect from `effectiveAt`. 0 reads as `1e18`. |
| `effectiveAt` | `uint64` | Timestamp at which `newUIMultiplier` takes effect. |
| `contractURI` | `string` | ERC-7572 metadata URI. Default empty. |
| `extraMetadata` | `mapping(string => string)` | Issuer-defined metadata entries. Default empty. |

Each portal adds a `cancelled` flag per asset and the count of distinct payout tokens distributed per asset. Each zone adds, per asset, a cumulative per-unit index for each payout token, and, per holder, a settled index and a claimable amount for each payout token.

# Tooling

- **Contract bindings:** add the new token functions to `ITIP20`, the factory overload, and the portal and zone additions to `tempo-contracts` and the SDKs.
- **Explorers and wallets:** read `decimals()` instead of assuming 6. Show `balanceOfUI` next to `balanceOf` when `supportsInterface(0xa60bf13d)` is true and `uiMultiplier() != 1e18`, and show pending updates from `newUIMultiplier()` and `effectiveAt()`. Refresh `name` and `symbol` on `NameUpdated` and `SymbolUpdated`.
- **Zone wallets:** show `pendingDistribution` for each held asset, and offer `claimDistributions`.
- **Issuer tooling:** a snapshot helper that lists Tempo holders at a block, excludes portals and protected addresses, and lists the portals that hold the asset.
- **`tips/verify`:** extend the TIP-20 reference implementation and invariant suite, and add a zone distribution model.

# Threat Model

- **`DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` holder:** fully trusted by holders. Can grant every role, change the transfer policy, rename the token, and change its metadata, including the ISIN. A rename can make a token resemble another; integrators SHOULD identify tokens by address.
- **`FREEZE_ROLE` holder:** trusted to hold funds only when legally required. Can freeze any unprotected Tempo account, including while paused. Cannot move funds.
- **`FORCED_TRANSFER_ROLE` holder:** trusted to move funds only when legally required. Can move any unprotected Tempo balance, and any zone holder's balance through `forceWithdraw`, including while paused. Cannot touch protected addresses or bypass the recipient's transfer or receive policy on Tempo.
- **`CORPORATE_ACTION_ROLE` holder:** trusted to record corporate actions correctly. A wrong multiplier misleads displays and pricing, but changes no balance. A wrong distribution rate misprices a payout. The portal pulls the funding up front, and refunds any excess to `refundRecipient`. Cancelling a zone holder's asset also needs `BURN_AT_ROLE`.
- **`BURN_AT_ROLE` holder:** trusted to destroy holdings only when legally required. With `CORPORATE_ACTION_ROLE`, can cancel every zone holder's balance of the asset through a cancelling distribution.
- **Zone operator:** sees every zone balance, as today. Cannot change token-level state, which the zone reads from Tempo, and cannot credit more of a payout token than the portal holds.
- **Holders:** untrusted. Cannot move frozen tokens, including through a receive-policy redirect, a zone deposit, or fee payment. Cannot claim a distribution for balance acquired after its record point.
- **Integrators and DeFi contracts:** must treat `balanceOf` as raw units, read `decimals()`, and apply `uiMultiplier()` only for display or pricing.

# Invariants

1. The sum of all balances equals `totalSupply()`.
2. `setUIMultiplier`, `cancelUIMultiplierUpdate`, `setFrozenTokens`, `setName`, `setSymbol`, `setContractURI`, and `setExtraMetadata` never change any `balanceOf` or `totalSupply()`.
3. A holder-initiated outflow of `amount` from `a`, including a zone deposit and fee payment, succeeds only if `amount <= unfrozen(a)`.
4. `frozen(a)` changes only through `setFrozenTokens`, `forcedTransfer`, `burnAt`, and `burnBlocked`. The last three only decrease it, by exactly the part of the debit that exceeded `unfrozen(a)`.
5. Protected addresses always have `frozen = 0`, and `forcedTransfer` never debits or credits them.
6. `0 < uiMultiplier() <= MAX_UI_MULTIPLIER` at all times.
7. `decimals()` is 6 or 18 and never changes. No token with 18 decimals has currency `USD`.
8. If `canTransfer(from, to, amount)` is `true`, a `transfer` or `transferFrom` of `amount` does not revert for pause, transfer-policy, or frozen-balance reasons.
9. A zone's name, symbol, decimals, multiplier, and metadata for a token equal Tempo's at the zone's anchor.
10. For each distribution, the payout credited in the zone never exceeds `ceil(rate * S / 1e18)`, and that amount plus the refund equals the funding.
11. A holder's entitlement from a distribution depends only on their balance at the record point.
12. After a cancelling distribution, the zone's supply of the asset is zero and stays zero.
13. A token that never uses this TIP's functions behaves exactly as it did before activation.
