# TIP-1180: Zone Identity

## Abstract

From the T15 hardfork, a Zone predeploy, `ZoneIdentity`, groups Zone accounts into orgs. Each org's admin decides, per TIP-20, which orgs its members may send to and receive from on the Zone, and which Tempo addresses they may deposit from and withdraw to. Zone execution checks every transfer, deposit and withdrawal against these rules, so several companies can share one Zone without funds leaving the paths they configured. Rules restrict counterparties only, not amounts or calls, and an org's admin and the readers it names may read its members' state.

## Motivation

A Zone restricts participants today only through its portal's account allowlist, which applies to the whole Zone. Companies sharing a Zone need rules per company, kept inside the Zone and enforced in execution, so the batch proof shows that only allowed moves happened. A sequencer that filters transactions misses transfers that contracts make in nested calls and binds only an honest sequencer.

TIP-403 policies are public on Tempo and restrict who holds a token, not who pays whom. The rule a company operating its users' accounts needs is which counterparties may receive or send which token.

On a Zone, only an account can read its own state. A company operating its users' accounts needs to read their balances, and often a delegate such as a custodian or compliance provider needs the same view without holding the admin key.

## Assumptions

- The Zone keeps the [privacy modifications](../zones/specs/spec.md#privacy-modifications) of the Zones specification. The sequencer sees everything and is trusted for liveness only.
- Org admins are operated or vetted by the Zone operator. Joining an org does not require the account's consent, so an admin can add any account that is in no org (see [Permission model](#permission-model)).
- Members have no exit their org cannot block.
- A Zone's T15 activation matches Tempo's. Zone chainspecs inherit Tempo's schedule unless their genesis overrides it.
- The portal admin may be a multisig on Tempo and never sends Zone transactions.

# Specification

## Overview

An **org** is an ID with an admin. Every Zone address is in at most one org; an address in no org is in **org 0**, which has no admin.

Each org has, per TIP-20:

- **Edges** to other orgs. An edge from org A to org B has two halves: A's admin sets the outbound half and B's admin sets the inbound half. A Zone transfer from A to B needs both. An org's edge to itself governs transfers between its members. Org 0 always agrees, so a transfer to or from org 0 needs only the other org's half, and transfers within org 0 are allowed.
- **L1 rules**: **targets**, Tempo addresses its members accept deposits from or may withdraw to, and a **policy** that opens either direction to any Tempo address. Tempo addresses are never members, so naming one binds only the naming org, and several orgs may name the same address.

The Zone's `ZonePortal` on Tempo holds the configuration: its admin sets the **registrar**, which creates orgs and appoints their admins, and turns enforcement on for the whole Zone. Until then, every move is allowed. The portal admin can also abdicate a capability ([TIP-1160](./tip-1160.md)), after which the setters it gates revert permanently; `AccessPolicy` is the capability that gates access configuration, including the identity configuration.

## Permission model

| Role | Set by | Can | Cannot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portal admin | Existing portal controls on Tempo | Set the registrar and turn enforcement on or off with `setIdentityConfig` | Act on the Zone; change either after abdicating `AccessPolicy` |
| Registrar | Portal admin | Create orgs, replace an org's admin, read any org's configuration and any account's org | Change members, edges or L1 rules; act once `AccessPolicy` is abdicated |
| Org admin | Registrar | Add accounts in no org, remove its members, set its halves of edges and its L1 rules, name readers | Act for another org or org 0; open a path the other org hasn't accepted |
| Reader | Org admin | Read the org's members' account-scoped state and the org's events | Change anything |
| Member | Its org admin | Use its own funds within its org's rules | Leave its org or change its rules |

By design:

- **No consent to join.** An admin can add any account in no org. Once added, the account is readable by the org, including its history, and can move funds only where the org allows, which may be nowhere. A later TIP will require the account to sign, or otherwise bind it to its org, before it joins.
- **No exit.** Only the admin can remove a member. A removed member is in org 0: it may withdraw to any Tempo address, but receives no deposits, and can transfer on the Zone only within org 0 or to orgs that set the inbound half of their edge from org 0.
- **The registrar can take over an org** by replacing its admin. Abdicating `AccessPolicy` on the portal ends every registrar power, after which no orgs can be created and a lost admin cannot be replaced.

## Mechanics

### Activation and configuration

At the first T15 block, the Zone deploys code at `ZoneIdentity`'s address, `0x1c00000000000000000000000000000000000003`, so its storage persists. Before T15, no check runs and the enforcement flag is not read.

`ZonePortal.setIdentityConfig(registrar, enforced)` is callable by the portal admin while the `AccessPolicy` capability is active. It stores both values packed after `transfersEnabled` in portal slot 28 and emits `IdentityConfigUpdated`. The Zone reads them from finalized Tempo state at its anchor. Once the portal's `AccessPolicy` abdication has taken effect, the Zone treats the registrar as unset, so every registrar call reverts with `Unauthorized`, comparing the abdication time with the Zone block's timestamp, which is never behind the anchored Tempo block's.

### Orgs and members

- `createOrg(admin)`: registrar only. Org IDs start at 1 and increase.
- `setOrgAdmin(orgId, admin)`: registrar only; refused for org 0.
- `addMembers(orgId, accounts)`: that org's admin only. Every account must be nonzero, not a [TIP-1022](./tip-1022.md) virtual address, not a Zone precompile such as a TIP-20 token, `ZoneInbox`, `ZoneOutbox` or `ZoneIdentity`, and in no org; otherwise the call adds none.
- `removeMember(account)`: the account's org admin only. Moves it to org 0. Every other caller, including for an account in org 0, gets the same `Unauthorized`.

### Edges and L1 rules

- `setEdge(fromOrg, toOrg, tokens, allowed)` sets, for each token, the halves the caller administers: the outbound half as `fromOrg`'s admin, the inbound half as `toOrg`'s. An admin of both orgs, as for an org's edge to itself, sets both. Nobody sets org 0's half. Both orgs must exist.
- `setL1Target(orgId, target, tokens, depositFrom, withdrawTo)` and `setL1Policy(orgId, tokens, policy)`: that org's admin only, so org 0 has no L1 rules. A target may be any nonzero address, including a virtual one, which is matched as given.

### The check

With enforcement on, a move of token T from `from` to `to` is allowed when:

| Move | Allowed when |
|---|---|
| Zone transfer | The edge from `org(from)` to `org(to)` for T has both halves set, counting org 0's half as set |
| Deposit from Tempo address `from` | `org(to)` is not 0, and its policy for T opens deposits or `from` is its target with `depositFrom` |
| Withdrawal to Tempo address `to` | `org(from)` is 0, or its policy for T opens withdrawals, or `to` is its target with `withdrawTo` |

Addresses are checked as given; virtual addresses are not resolved to their master. Zone TIP-20 already rejects virtual recipients, and a withdrawal to a virtual Tempo address needs that exact address as a target or an open policy.

The check applies at:

| Operation | Pair checked | When denied |
|---|---|---|
| TIP-20 `transfer`, `transferWithMemo` | caller to `to` | Reverts with `OrgPolicyForbids` |
| TIP-20 `transferFrom`, `transferFromWithMemo` | `from` to `to`, only when the amount is nonzero and the caller's allowance covers it | Reverts with `OrgPolicyForbids`; otherwise TIP-20 answers as usual |
| Deposit | Tempo sender to the decrypted recipient | Bounces back to Tempo |
| `ZoneOutbox.requestWithdrawal` | sender to the Tempo `to` as a withdrawal, and sender to `zoneFallbackRecipient` as a Zone transfer when it differs | Reverts with `OrgPolicyForbids` |

The TIP-20 check runs in the token precompile after the T15 and `transfersEnabled` checks, so it covers nested calls. Fees, burns and withdrawal bounce-backs are not checked; a bounce-back follows the decision made when the withdrawal was requested. Checking `transferFrom` only under a covering allowance keeps a spender without one from learning the owner's edges.

### Withdrawal sender org

With enforcement on, the outbox records the sender's org on each withdrawal and emits `WithdrawalSenderOrg` when it is not 0. `Withdrawal` gains a `senderOrg` field. A withdrawal with `senderOrg == 0` hashes into the withdrawal queue exactly as before T15, so withdrawals queued before T15 dequeue unchanged; any other withdrawal hashes with `senderOrg`. During a withdrawal callback, `ZonePortal.callbackSenderOrg()` returns the withdrawal's `senderOrg`, and 0 at any other time, so a receiver must check that it was called by the messenger, the contract shared by all Zones through which portals relay withdrawal callbacks, and read `callbackSenderOrg` from the `sourcePortal` it passes. The sequencer submits the pre-T15 or T15 `processWithdrawals` encoding according to the active Tempo hardfork.

### Reads

`canRead(reader, account)` holds when `reader` is `account`, or when `account` is in an org other than 0 and `reader` is that org's current admin or one of its readers. The org's admin names and removes readers with `setReader(orgId, reader, allowed)`. Readers belong to the org, so replacing the admin keeps them; the new admin finds them through the org's `ReaderUpdated` events and removes those it doesn't trust.

From T15, precompile getters that select state by account (TIP-20 `balanceOf`, `allowance` and permit nonces, nonce lanes, keychain and storage-credit getters, `ZoneInbox.refunds` and `ReceivePolicyGuard.balanceOf`) also admit a caller for which `canRead` holds for the account, or for any party of `allowance` or of the receipt. The redacted RPC applies the same rule, evaluated at the latest block, to `eth_getBalance`, `eth_getTransactionCount`, lookups of transactions the account sent and their receipts, and log queries and subscriptions naming the account. It also serves an org's `ZoneIdentity` events to the org's current admin and readers, for log queries whose only address is `ZoneIdentity` and that name the org in the event's org topic. When `eth_call` or `eth_estimateGas` runs at a block other than `latest` or `pending`, it fails with `Unauthorized` unless every read `canRead(msg.sender, account)` granted it still holds at the latest block. Because the rule is evaluated at the latest block, removing a reader or replacing an admin also revokes their reads of members' state at earlier blocks. The Zones specification gives the full RPC behavior.

### Gas

A caller sees its own gas, so gas must not depend on another account's org or on an org's rules:

- Each check reads the same slots whatever the answer: both members and one edge for a transfer, one member plus the org's policy and target entry for a deposit or withdrawal. `ZoneIdentity` prices every storage read as cold.
- TIP-20 transfers, `transferFrom` and `approve` cost a fixed amount on a Zone, including when they revert with `OrgPolicyForbids`.
- `canRead`, `orgOf` and `removeMember` read the same slots whatever the answer, so an outsider gets the same answer at the same cost for members and non-members.

## Interfaces

```solidity
interface IZoneIdentity {
    /// Tempo addresses an org's members may use for one token without a target.
    struct L1Policy {
        bool openDeposit;
        bool openWithdrawal;
    }

    /// Creates an org administered by `admin`. Registrar only.
    function createOrg(address admin) external returns (uint64 orgId);
    /// Replaces an org's admin, keeping its readers. Registrar only; reverts for org 0.
    function setOrgAdmin(uint64 orgId, address admin) external;

    /// Adds accounts in no org to `orgId`, all or none. That org's admin only.
    function addMembers(uint64 orgId, address[] calldata accounts) external;
    /// Moves a member to org 0. Its org's admin only.
    function removeMember(address account) external;
    /// Sets, for each token, the halves of the edge from `fromOrg` to `toOrg` the caller administers.
    function setEdge(uint64 fromOrg, uint64 toOrg, address[] calldata tokens, bool allowed) external;
    /// Sets whether `orgId`'s members accept deposits from and may withdraw to `target`, per token.
    function setL1Target(uint64 orgId, address target, address[] calldata tokens, bool depositFrom, bool withdrawTo) external;
    /// Sets `orgId`'s policy for each token.
    function setL1Policy(uint64 orgId, address[] calldata tokens, L1Policy policy) external;
    /// Names or removes a reader of `orgId`'s members. That org's admin only.
    function setReader(uint64 orgId, address reader, bool allowed) external;

    /// Whether `canRead(msg.sender, account)` holds.
    function canRead(address account) external view returns (bool);
    /// Zero for org 0.
    function orgAdmin(uint64 orgId) external view returns (address);
    /// For callers that `canRead` the account, and the registrar.
    function orgOf(address account) external view returns (uint64 orgId);
    /// For the admin of either org and the registrar.
    function edge(uint64 fromOrg, uint64 toOrg, address token) external view returns (bool allowed);
    /// For the org's admin and the registrar.
    function l1Target(uint64 orgId, address target, address token) external view returns (bool depositFrom, bool withdrawTo);
    /// For the org's admin and the registrar.
    function l1Policy(uint64 orgId, address token) external view returns (L1Policy policy);

    event OrgCreated(uint64 indexed orgId, address admin);
    event OrgAdminUpdated(uint64 indexed orgId, address admin);
    event MemberAdded(address indexed account, uint64 indexed orgId);
    event MemberRemoved(address indexed account, uint64 indexed orgId);
    event EdgeUpdated(uint64 indexed fromOrg, uint64 indexed toOrg, address indexed token, bool fromOk, bool toOk);
    event L1TargetUpdated(uint64 indexed orgId, address indexed target, address indexed token, bool depositFrom, bool withdrawTo);
    event L1PolicyUpdated(uint64 indexed orgId, address indexed token, L1Policy policy);
    event ReaderUpdated(uint64 indexed orgId, address indexed reader, bool allowed);

    /// The caller is not the required admin or registrar, or there is none.
    error Unauthorized();
    /// `orgId` is above the highest org created.
    error UnknownOrg(uint64 orgId);
    /// A zero, virtual or precompile address, or org 0 where an org is required.
    error InvalidAddress();
    /// `addMembers` for an account already in an org.
    error AlreadyMember(address account);
    /// The move fails the check.
    error OrgPolicyForbids(address token, address from, address to);
}

interface IZonePortal {
    /// Sets the ZoneIdentity registrar and enforcement flag. Admin only, while AccessPolicy is active.
    function setIdentityConfig(address registrar, bool enforced) external;
    /// Sender org of the withdrawal whose callback is running; 0 otherwise.
    function callbackSenderOrg() external view returns (uint64);
    event IdentityConfigUpdated(address registrar, bool enforced);
}

interface IZoneOutbox {
    event WithdrawalSenderOrg(uint64 indexed withdrawalIndex, uint64 senderOrg);
}
```

`Withdrawal` appends `uint64 senderOrg` after `encryptedSender`, and `processWithdrawals` takes the extended struct from T15.

`ZoneIdentity` storage, in field order:

| Field | Type |
|---|---|
| `org_count` | `uint64` |
| `orgs` | `mapping(uint64 => Org)` |
| `members` | `mapping(address => uint64)`, the account's org |

`Org` is `{ admin: address, edges: mapping(uint64 toOrg => mapping(address token => Edge)), l1: mapping(address token => L1Rules), readers: mapping(address => bool) }`. `Edge` is `{ from_ok, to_ok }` in one slot. `L1Rules` is `{ policy: L1Policy, targets: mapping(address => { deposit_from, withdraw_to }) }`; the policy and each target take one slot.

The portal stores `_identityRegistrar` at slot 28, offset 10, and `_identityEnforced` at slot 28, offset 30, without getters, to stay under the code size limit. The outbox stores a pending withdrawal's `sender_org` in the slot already holding its `gas_limit` and `fallback_nonce`, so pending withdrawals keep their pre-T15 size.

# Compatibility

- **Existing Zones:** unchanged until T15 and until the portal admin enables enforcement. Reads change only for accounts in an org.
- **Before enabling enforcement:** create orgs, add members, and set edges and L1 rules. Deposit senders and withdrawal destinations must be targets or covered by a policy, or deposits bounce and withdrawals revert.
- **Withdrawal consumers:** `processWithdrawals` callers, provers and indexers that decode `Withdrawal` need the T15 encoding. Callback receivers that don't call `callbackSenderOrg` are unaffected.
- **Indexers:** mappings cannot be listed; views of orgs, members, edges, L1 rules and readers are rebuilt from `ZoneIdentity` events. The redacted RPC serves an org's events only to its admin and readers.

# Observability

| Event | Answers |
|---|---|
| `OrgCreated`, `OrgAdminUpdated` | Which orgs exist and who administers each |
| `MemberAdded`, `MemberRemoved` | Who joined and left each org |
| `EdgeUpdated` | Both halves of an edge for a token after each change |
| `L1TargetUpdated`, `L1PolicyUpdated` | An org's Tempo rules for a token |
| `ReaderUpdated` | The org's readers, named or removed by its admins |
| `WithdrawalSenderOrg` | The org a withdrawal was requested from |
| `IdentityConfigUpdated` (Tempo) | When the registrar or enforcement changed |

A denied move shows as an `OrgPolicyForbids` revert naming the token and both addresses, or as a deposit bounce-back.

# Threat Model

The most consequential attack is a compromised org admin key: the attacker can add any account in no org, trap its funds by opening no edges, and read its state, without the account's consent.

| Actor | If compromised or malicious | Bounded by |
|---|---|---|
| Portal admin | Turns enforcement off or replaces the registrar | Existing portal controls; abdicating `AccessPolicy` freezes both |
| Registrar | Creates orgs, takes over any org by replacing its admin, reads any org's configuration | Cannot move funds; loses every power once `AccessPolicy` is abdicated |
| Org admin | Adds accounts in no org, traps members' funds, reads members, names readers | Cannot set another org's half or org 0's rules; cannot touch other orgs' members. Its readers outlive its replacement until the new admin removes them |
| Reader | Reads members' state and the org's events | Removed by the admin, which also revokes its reads of past blocks |
| Sequencer | Delays transactions | Checks run in execution and are proven |
| Any account | Learns from its own failed transfer whether the recipient's org accepts it | Cannot change rules; `transferFrom` reveals edges only to spenders with a covering allowance |

Visible by design: whether a move would be allowed, to whoever attempts it, since a denial must fail; that an address is in some org, to an admin whose `addMembers` fails; each withdrawal's `senderOrg`, on Tempo.

# Invariants

- With enforcement on, every Zone TIP-20 balance change other than fees, burns and withdrawal bounce-backs, every deposit credit and every withdrawal request satisfies [the check](#the-check) in the state before it.
- An address is in at most one org, and virtual addresses and Zone precompiles are in none.
- An edge's outbound half changes only through its sending org's admin, its inbound half only through its receiving org's admin, and org 0's half never changes.
- Only an org's admin changes its members, its halves of edges, its L1 rules or its readers; only the registrar creates orgs or changes an org's admin; once `AccessPolicy` abdication takes effect, the registrar does neither.
- `canRead(r, a)` holds only if `r == a`, or `r` is the current admin or a reader of `a`'s org; for an account in org 0, only if `r == a`.
- A withdrawal with `senderOrg == 0` hashes exactly as before T15.
- Before T15, execution reads neither `ZoneIdentity` storage nor the enforcement flag. With enforcement off, every move is allowed.
- The gas of a check, and of `canRead`, `orgOf` and `removeMember` for an outsider, does not depend on the orgs, edges, L1 rules or membership involved.

Tests must cover the T14 to T15 transition with orgs configured after it; edges opening only once both halves are set, and per token; one-sided edges to and from org 0; deposits from targets, open policies and unknown senders, and into org 0; withdrawals to targets, open policies, unknown destinations and from org 0; `transferFrom` with no, insufficient and covering allowance; the fallback recipient check; `senderOrg` in the queue hash and `callbackSenderOrg` during and after a callback, including after the receiver deposits back; the registrar losing its powers at abdication; equal gas across allowed and denied checks; reads by an admin, a named reader, a revoked reader and an outsider, including a revoked reader's `eth_call` at a block before the revocation; readers kept across an admin replacement; and org events served to the org's admin and readers only.
