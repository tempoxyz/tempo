# TIP-1160: Zone TIP-20 Transfers and L1 Pause Mirroring

## Abstract

Before T15, Zone TIP-20 balances move only through the protocol paths: deposits credited by
`ZoneInbox` and withdrawals burned by `ZoneOutbox`. From T15, a Zone's portal admin can opt the
Zone into zone-internal `transfer` and `transferFrom` with a new `ZonePortal.transfersEnabled`
flag, which the Zone reads from finalized Tempo state. T15 also mirrors each TIP-20 token's Tempo
pause flag into Zone execution, so a token its issuer pauses on Tempo is paused on every Zone.
Sequencer-local allowlists for transfer parties are out of scope: they are block-builder policy,
not consensus.

## Motivation

Zones are useful for private payments only if value can move between Zone accounts without
leaving the Zone. Today every Zone TIP-20 transfer reverts with `Unauthorized`, so a payment
needs a withdrawal to Tempo and a fresh deposit.

Enabling transfers changes what an existing Zone guarantees to its operator and to issuers. Funds
that could previously only enter and leave through the portal can now change hands inside the
Zone, where only the sequencer and prover see them. Transfers are therefore opt-in per Zone, and
the decision sits with the portal admin, who already controls the Zone's access policy.

Transfers also expose a gap in how the Zone follows issuer controls. The Zone mirrors the TIP-403
registry from Tempo, so policy changes reach the Zone, but each token's `paused` slot is
Zone-local storage that nothing writes: `pause` and `unpause` are blocked on the Zone. A token
its issuer paused on Tempo stays live on every Zone. With transfers enabled, paused funds could
keep circulating. Mirroring the flag closes that gap with the mechanism the Zone already uses for
TIP-403 state.

Alternatives considered:

- **Enable transfers on every Zone at T15.** Rejected: it changes existing Zones' guarantees
  without their operators' consent.
- **Gate transfers only with a sequencer allowlist.** Rejected as the sole control: block
  validation and the prover do not replicate builder policy, so it cannot be a protocol
  guarantee. It stays available as an additional local control
  ([tempoxyz/tempo#8634](https://github.com/tempoxyz/tempo/pull/8634)).
- **Copy the pause flag into Zone storage during `advanceTempo`**, the system call that opens
  each Zone block by importing finalized Tempo headers. Rejected: it needs a system write for every token on every import. The Zone's database adapter already answers TIP-403
  registry reads from Tempo state at the anchor; routing the pause slot through it costs nothing
  until a call reads the slot.

## Assumptions

- Zones read Tempo state at the finalized anchor recorded in `TempoState`. A
  `setTransfersEnabled` call or an issuer's pause takes effect on a Zone only once the Zone has
  imported the Tempo block that recorded it. Until then the Zone follows the previous value.
- The portal admin is trusted to decide whether its Zone allows transfers. Abdicating
  `Capability.AccessPolicy` (giving up the right to change the Zone's access policy, effective
  after a fixed delay) freezes the flag at its current value.
- Contract creation is disabled on Zones, so Permit2 and Multicall3 are the only predeploys that
  relay arbitrary callers. If that changes, the relay rules below must be revisited.
- The Zone admits transfers only under its own fork schedule (`t15Time` in its genesis), while
  the flag lives in Tempo storage. A Zone whose genesis does not schedule T15 rejects transfers
  even after its portal opts in, so operators schedule T15 on the Zone no earlier than on Tempo.

# Specification

## Overview

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Zone token | A TIP-20 token enabled on a Zone, deployed at the same address as on Tempo. |
| Anchor | The finalized Tempo block number recorded in `TempoState.tempoBlockNumber`. |
| Portal admin | The `ZonePortal` admin of the Zone. |
| Relay predeploys | Permit2 (`PERMIT2_ADDRESS`) and Multicall3 (`MULTICALL3_ADDRESS`). |
| Transfer-family call | `transfer`, `transferWithMemo`, `transferFrom` and `transferFromWithMemo` on a Zone token. |

Only the portal admin authorizes a Zone's transfers, on Tempo. The Zone sequencer cannot enable
them, and block validation re-executes the admission check, so a sequencer cannot include a
transfer that the Zone's flag forbids.

## Mechanics

### 1. T15 portal runtime

All Zone portals on Tempo delegate to one shared `ZonePortal` implementation at
`ZONE_PORTAL_IMPL_ADDRESS`, installed by the enshrined ZoneFactory (TIP-1091) together with the
shared batch verifier and messenger. T13 replaced all three runtimes (TIP-1098); T15 replaces only
the portal's.

At the first Tempo block where T15 is active, the node installs the T15 portal runtime
(`T15_ZONE_PORTAL_RUNTIME`) at `ZONE_PORTAL_IMPL_ADDRESS` and leaves its storage unchanged. The
verifier and messenger keep their T13 runtimes. Because the T15 portal builds on the T13 one, the
node installs the newest runtimes whose fork and every earlier runtime fork are active: a chain
that has T15 at genesis but activates T13 later installs the T15 portal when T13 activates. Chains
with both forks at genesis ship the T15 runtime in their genesis allocation.

The runtime is a pinned constant, not a build output, because it becomes consensus state at the
boundary. It MUST match a `forge build` of `ZonePortal.sol` with the pinned compiler until T15
activates on mainnet.

### 2. Transfer opt-in

`ZonePortal` gains `bool transfersEnabled`, packed into slot 28 at byte offset 9, after
`tokenEnablementCursorInitialized`. It defaults to `false`, so every existing portal starts
opted out.

`setTransfersEnabled(bool enabled)`:

1. Reverts with `NotAdmin` unless `msg.sender` is the portal admin.
2. Reverts with `CapabilityAbdicated(AccessPolicy)` once an `AccessPolicy` abdication is effective.
3. Writes `transfersEnabled = enabled` and emits `TransfersEnabledUpdated(enabled)`, even when the
   value is unchanged.

The call does not check the portal's pause state.

### 3. Transfer admission on the Zone

The Zone's TIP-20 rules run before the call reaches the upstream TIP-20 precompile. For a
transfer-family call with `caller`, `from` and `to` (`from = caller` for `transfer` and
`transferWithMemo`), the checks run in this order:

1. Before T15: revert with `Unauthorized`.
2. If `caller`, `from` or `to` is a relay predeploy: revert with `Unauthorized`. Relays cannot
   spend allowances granted to them, have their own balance moved, or receive funds they could
   never send on.
3. If the Zone portal's `transfersEnabled` at the anchor is `false`: revert with `Unauthorized`.
4. If `to` is a TIP-1022 virtual address, resolve it to its master address.
   If the recipient's TIP-1028 receive policy blocks
   `from`: revert with `PolicyForbids`. On Tempo the funds would be diverted to
   `ReceivePolicyGuard` instead; on a Zone the policy's recovery authority may be an address that
   cannot act there, such as an L1 contract, and could never claim them.
5. Forward the call unchanged to the upstream TIP-20 precompile, which applies the TIP-403
   sender and recipient policies (mirrored from Tempo), the pause check (section 4), the
   allowance, and the balance check.

The fixed 100,000 gas charge for transfer-family calls and `approve` is unchanged.

From T15, two relay rules apply outside transfers:

- `approve` reverts with `Unauthorized` when the caller is a relay predeploy, since anyone can make
  Multicall3 approve a spender for its own balance.
- `ZoneOutbox.requestWithdrawal` reverts when the caller is a relay predeploy, so a relay cannot
  move Zone funds through the outbox's internal `transferFrom` either.

### 4. L1 pause mirroring

From T15, Zone execution reads the `paused` slot (`tip20::slots::PAUSED`) of any TIP-20-prefixed
address from Tempo state at the anchor, not from Zone storage. Like a TIP-403 registry read, the
EVM's `SLOAD` charge covers it, with no extra host charge. The Zone never writes this slot:
`pause` and `unpause` stay blocked for every caller.

A token paused on Tempo therefore blocks on the Zone exactly what it blocks on Tempo:

| Zone operation | Effect while the token is paused on Tempo |
|---|---|
| Transfer-family calls | Revert with `ContractPaused` |
| Deposit (`ZoneInbox` mint) | Reverts, so the deposit bounces back to Tempo |
| `requestWithdrawal` (burn) | Reverts |
| Transactions paying fees in the token | Invalid |
| `approve`, `permit`, reads | Unaffected |

Before T15, the Zone reads the Zone-local slot, which is always unpaused.

## Interfaces

```solidity
interface IZonePortal {
    /// @notice Emitted when zone-internal TIP-20 transfers are enabled or disabled.
    event TransfersEnabledUpdated(bool enabled);

    /// @notice Whether zone-internal TIP-20 transfers are enabled. Defaults to false.
    function transfersEnabled() external view returns (bool);

    /// @notice Enable or disable zone-internal TIP-20 transfers.
    /// @dev Only callable by the admin. Reverts with NotAdmin for any other caller and with
    ///      CapabilityAbdicated(AccessPolicy) once an AccessPolicy abdication is effective.
    function setTransfersEnabled(bool enabled) external;
}
```

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| `transfersEnabled()` | `0xbef97c87` |
| `setTransfersEnabled(bool)` | `0x26b9ce13` |
| `TransfersEnabledUpdated(bool)` topic | `0xdb41d2f25c62ff78fc21d2ee04045d19649e1a120537236682b9688ddd4eb27c` |
| Storage | slot 28, offset 9, `bool` |

No Zone TIP-20 interface changes. The errors already exist: `Unauthorized`, `PolicyForbids`,
`ContractPaused`.

# Compatibility

| Integration | What changes |
|---|---|
| Existing Zones | Nothing until the portal admin calls `setTransfersEnabled(true)`. Transfers keep reverting with `Unauthorized`. |
| Zone users and wallets | Can send TIP-20 within an opted-in Zone. A transfer may revert while the opt-in waits for the Zone to import the block that recorded it. Wallets should read `transfersEnabled()` on the portal before offering transfers. |
| Issuers | A Tempo pause now also pauses the token on every Zone, including deposits and withdrawals. |
| Permit2 and Multicall3 users on Zones | Cannot move Zone funds or approve spenders from T15. |
| Zone node operators | Must run a T15-capable release before T15 activates. The L1 state cache invalidates a token's cached pause flag on its `PauseStateUpdate` event. |
| Zone checker | Ignores `TransfersEnabledUpdated`; it does not change L1 liabilities. |
| Sequencer allowlist | Optional and builder-local; see [Sequencer transfer allowlist](#sequencer-transfer-allowlist-non-normative). |
| Tempo shadow replay | Expects only the portal's code to change at the T15 boundary. |

## Sequencer transfer allowlist (non-normative)

Sequencers can restrict transfers further with the deposit allowlist
([tempoxyz/tempo#8634](https://github.com/tempoxyz/tempo/pull/8634)). This is block-builder
policy: Zone followers and the prover accept any block that passes the rules above, so it adds
no consensus rule and needs no hardfork. With `--deposits.allowlist` and a written list:

- Submission rejects a transaction when any of its calls, including each call of an AA batch, is
  a transfer-family call whose `from` or `to` is not listed, or a `transferFrom` by an unlisted
  caller. The error is `transfer sender or recipient is not allowlisted`.
- Submission rejects any call to Multicall3 while the allowlist is enabled, even before the list
  is written. Read-only `eth_call`s through it still work.
- While leading, the sequencer executes each pool transaction and leaves it out of the block, and
  drops it from its pool, when any TIP-20 `Transfer` it emits names an unlisted `from` or `to`.
- Transfers to `ZoneOutbox` and to the block's fee recipient are exempt, so an account removed
  from the list can still pay fees and withdraw.
- Every RPC follower mirrors the leader's list in memory, with no flag: at startup, after each
  write it relays, and every second. It rejects the same transactions on submission and evicts
  pooled ones the new list rejects. While it holds no written list it admits every transfer.
- Without a written list, the sequencer admits every transfer. Simulations (`eth_call`,
  `eth_estimateGas`) do not check the list.

# Observability

- `TransfersEnabledUpdated(bool enabled)` on the portal: when a Zone opted in or out, and who
  changed it (the transaction sender).
- The existing `PauseStateUpdate` event on the Tempo token shows when a pause starts and ends.
  Whether it has reached a Zone follows from the Zone's anchor in `TempoState`.
- Rejected transfers revert with `Unauthorized` (pre-T15, opted out, or relay),
  `PolicyForbids` (TIP-403 or receive policy) or `ContractPaused`. Each reason maps to one
  error.
- Zone nodes count L1 cache misses as `tempo_zone_l1_state_cache_misses`, labelled by `origin`
  (`canonical`, `prewarming`, `simulation`). Canonical misses show whether prewarming keeps
  transfer-related L1 reads (TIP-403 policies, pause flags, the portal flag) off the
  block-building path.

# Threat Model

The most consequential risk is funds moving inside a Zone after an issuer or operator has tried
to stop them. The pause mirror and the mirrored TIP-403 registry hold Zone transfers to the
issuer's current Tempo controls, delayed only by the anchor's lag.

| Actor | Controls | Enforcing check | If compromised or wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portal admin | The opt-in flag | `onlyAdmin`; `AccessPolicy` abdication freezes it | Can enable or disable transfers on its own Zone, not on others. |
| Sequencer | Ordering and inclusion | Validation and the prover re-run the admission checks | Can censor transfers, cannot admit forbidden ones. |
| Issuer | Pause, TIP-403 policies on Tempo | Mirrored reads at the anchor | Controls take effect after the anchor lag, not instantly. |
| Any user | Relay predeploys | Relay checks in section 3 | Without them, public Permit2 state and Multicall3's caller-as-self behavior would let anyone move relay-held funds. |
| Approved spender | `transferFrom` simulations | None (accepted) | Learns `min(balance, allowance)` of the owner. The owner bounds this by approving exact amounts. |

Residual risks:

- Simulations of transfers to arbitrary recipients cause uncached Tempo storage reads. The
  redacted RPC bounds concurrent simulations, and
  [tempoxyz/tempo#8722](https://github.com/tempoxyz/tempo/pull/8722) bounds what each can cost:
  outside canonical execution, a cache miss makes one L1 fetch limited to 5 seconds, and fails
  without a request while 256 such fetches are outstanding. The sequencer also prewarms the L1
  cache by simulating pool transactions and queued deposits before building, and portal reads
  behind `zone_getZoneInfo` and `zone_getEncryptionKey` are shared between callers for up to one
  second.
- After a pause, pooled transactions that pay fees in the paused token stay in the pool until a
  block rejects them.

# Invariants

- Before T15, every transfer-family call on a Zone token reverts with `Unauthorized`.
- From T15, no transfer-family call succeeds unless the portal's `transfersEnabled` at the anchor
  is `true`.
- No call moves funds into, out of, or on behalf of Permit2 or Multicall3 on a Zone from T15.
- No Zone transfer ever credits `ReceivePolicyGuard`.
- From T15, the Zone's view of a token's `paused` flag equals Tempo's at the anchor, and Zone
  execution never writes it.
- The T15 boundary changes only the portal runtime's code, not its storage, the verifier or the
  messenger.

Tests must cover: opted-out and opted-in transfers on both sides of T15, `setTransfersEnabled` by
a non-admin and after an `AccessPolicy` abdication, each relay position (caller, `from`, `to`),
receive-policy and TIP-403 rejections, deposits, withdrawals and transfers while the token is
paused on Tempo, and the runtime install for chains with T15 at genesis, at T13 activation, and at
a later T15 boundary.
