Added the TIP-1153 account reservations precompile interface (`IReservations`) and its `RESERVATIONS_ADDRESS`, a system precompile from T15: holds on TIP-20 balances, capture (including capture of a live hold by its payee, up to the held amount, and several non-final captures of one hold through `capturePart` and `captureForPart`), charge, capped debt, an overcapture tolerance the owner's root key sets per payee and token (`setCaptureTolerance`, `ToleranceExceeded`), stand-in holds and charges whose shortfall may become debt up to an offline cap the root key sets (`holdStandIn`, `chargeStandIn`, `setOfflineCap`, `offlineCap`, `StandInHoldPlaced`), linked refunds, and credit lines: a lender's offer accepted by the owner's root key reserves the lender's balance for the undrawn limit, holds put part of their amount on the line, captures draw it after the owner's balance and refunds and repayments repay it (`openLine`, `offerCredit`, `grantCredit`, `holdWithLine`, `holdCardWithLine` (TIP-1169: a line hold through a card, checked against it), `holdCardStandIn` (TIP-1169: a stand-in hold through a card, checked against its status and program but not its limit), `repayCredit`, `creditOf`, `getLine`, `creditOffer`, `LineExists`, `LineNotFound`, `CreditNotOffered` and the `Line*`/`Credit*` events).
